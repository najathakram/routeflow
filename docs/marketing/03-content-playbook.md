## 1. Brand voice

- **Operator-to-operator.** Write like someone who has loaded a van at 5am. Short sentences.
  Concrete nouns (cases, checks, licences, pallets) over abstractions ("efficiency,"
  "streamline").
- **Show the mechanism, not the adjective.** Not "powerful invoicing" — "the invoice matches
  what came off the truck."
- **Honest by default.** No invented numbers. If we can't prove it, we describe the mechanism
  instead of claiming the outcome.
- **Bilingual where the audience is.** Retailer-facing assets get Spanish variants; the product
  itself is EN/ES — say so.

## 3. What every asset must include (master checklist)

Every piece of content — video, post, flyer, brochure, ad — must contain:

1. **One audience** (distributor _or_ retailer — never both in one asset).
2. **One pain, named in the audience's words** (the hook).
3. **One mechanism shown** (a real screen or flow — the proof is the product).
4. **One benefit sentence** (what changes for them).
5. **One CTA** with a low-friction next step (QR/link; trial for distributors, "ask your
   supplier" for retailers).
6. **Brand block:** logo, tagline, URL `[routeflow domain]`.
7. **Claim check:** nothing from the do-not-market list (doc 01 §15); placeholders resolved;
   compliance footer if applicable.
8. For print: contact `[phone / email]`, QR code to `[signup or demo URL]`; for social: 3–5
   hashtags max, caption ≤ 3 lines before the fold.

### 4.2 Video concept bank (top 5 scripted, 5 more outlined)

**V1 — "Stop typing supplier invoices" (the flagship demo)**

- _Hook (0–2s):_ A thick stack of paper invoices slams on a desk. Text: "This used to be
  Sunday."
- _Script:_ "This is how a distributor enters a supplier invoice now. [Phone photographs the
  bill.] RouteFlow reads the supplier, the invoice number, every line — even the item codes and
  pack sizes. It matches them to my products, asks when it's not sure, and learns my supplier's
  descriptions. If I've already entered this bill, it stops me. I hit receive — and my stock
  and my costs just updated."
- _Shots:_ paper stack → phone camera capture → extraction review screen → "Did you mean…" chip
  tap → duplicate warning → receive → product cost/stock card.
- _CTA:_ "Scan your first bill free — link in bio."
- _Caption:_ "Photograph the bill. Stock and costs update themselves. #wholesale #distribution"

**V2 — "Build the order in 40 seconds" (split-screen scanner)**

- _Hook:_ "Watch me build a wholesale order faster than you can text one."
- _Script:_ "Camera on top, order on the bottom. [Scans item after item.] Every scan adds a
  line at the price I charged this store last time. Scan the case code, it adds a case; scan the
  unit code, it adds a piece. Wrong margin? It warns me before I give it away. Phone rings? I
  minimize the order and pick it back up later — nothing lost."
- _Shots:_ continuous split-screen scanning, price prefill, margin warning + "Set to floor,"
  minimize to draft dock, resume.
- _CTA:_ trial link.

**V3 — "The bounced check"**

- _Hook:_ "A customer's check just bounced. Here's what my software did about it."
- _Script:_ "I recorded the check when Marcus brought it in — with a photo. It went to
  deposited, then the bank said NSF. One tap: the payment is voided, the invoice reopens on its
  own, the NSF fee is added, and the store sees the reopened balance in their portal. Nothing
  slips through."
- _Shots:_ payment w/ photo → status chips (deposited → bounced) → invoice reopening → buyer
  portal view of the reopened balance.
- _CTA:_ "Get paid what you're owed — link in bio."

**V4 — "Filing day, in minutes" (regulated wedge)**

- _Hook:_ "Excise filing used to take my weekend. Watch."
- _Script:_ "Every regulated sale I made this period is already in the ledger — including the
  returns and voids, netted correctly. I open the period, hit Prepare Filing, and download the
  report in my state's format — permit numbers, retailer licence numbers, all of it. If a
  licence number is missing, it warns me _before_ I file."
- _Shots:_ compliance dashboard → ledger → prepare filing → warnings panel → CSV in the state
  layout.
- _CTA:_ demo link. _Include the compliance footer in the caption._

**V5 — "The store that never runs out" (buyer portal / retailer-facing)**

- _Hook (retailer POV):_ "My beverage guy gave me an app. Now I order before I run out."

- _Shots:_ Your Shelf (running low / due soon), add-all-low, order tracking steps, invoice list
  w/ check badge, statement PDF.
- _CTA:_ distributors: "Give your customers this — link in bio." Spanish variant priority #1.

**Outlined (produce as the calendar needs them):**

## 8. Demo script (15 minutes, stub-safe)

A path that shows only shipped features and lands the five pillars. Rehearse on the `test`
tenant with dummy data only (never a live client tenant).

1. **(2 min) The order** — open the order builder, scan 4–5 items with a phone/wedge, show the
   remembered price and a margin warning, add a below-floor line and "Set to floor." Minimize
   to the draft dock; resume. _Message: speed + guardrails._
2. **(2 min) The buyer's side** — switch to the buyer portal: Your Shelf running-low list,
   "Add all low," show the promo strikethrough in the cart. _Message: customers reorder
   themselves._
3. **(3 min) Delivery → invoice truth** — complete a stop with a short-pick (mobile or web
   my-runs), show the invoice rebuilt to delivered quantities; record an at-door check with a
   photo. _Message: the invoice matches the truck._
4. **(3 min) The bill scan** — photograph a (dummy) supplier invoice, walk the extraction,
   "Did you mean…", duplicate warning, receive; open the product to show cost/stock updated.
   _Message: stop typing._
5. **(3 min) Compliance** — attempt an order with an unlicensed regulated item (blocked),
   approve the licence, show the ledger and Prepare Filing → CSV in the state layout.
   _Message: sell regulated goods without fear._
6. **(2 min) The money view** — dashboard AR aging, a customer statement, P&L. Close on the
   trial CTA + migration story (CSV + batch scan + numbering continuity).

**Avoid in demos:** live map/GPS anything, messaging/inbox, integrations settings tab, pick &
load, driver cash-up screen, estimates email-send, FIFO/LIFO talk.
