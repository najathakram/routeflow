Security and your data

Who can see your data.

We’re a small, new service. This page says what we do, and what we don’t have.

What we do.

Three things, in plain words.

  • Businesses are kept apart

    Each business’s data is kept apart. Our app checks the business whenever it reads or writes your data. The check is in the app. It isn’t also enforced inside the database.

  • Sign-in has limits

    Passwords are stored as hashes, not as plain text. Ten wrong tries lock a login for 15 minutes. You can see your sign-ins and end them.

  • Files open by private link

    Photos and files open through private links that expire.

Who can do what.

There are four kinds of login. Each one reaches different things.

  • Admin

    Everything staff can do. Admins also change billing and settings, and add staff.

  • Staff

    Orders, customers, invoices, and stock.

  • Driver

    The route and the stops, in a web browser.

  • Store

    Its own prices, orders, and invoices, in a web browser.

Access goes by role. You can’t set it for one person. The server checks the role too, as well as the screen.

Who at RouteFlow can see your data.

To help you, our staff can sign in as the admin of your account.

  • How long. A support sign-in lasts 15 minutes.
  • What it can do. It can change your data, the way an admin can.
  • What it can’t do. It can’t change billing, payment setup, or your own sign-in details.
  • Is it recorded. Yes. We record the start of each support sign-in.

The details

What you can check, and what we don’t have.

We name the gaps instead of showing badges.

What can I check myself?

  • The edit history of an order
  • The payments on an invoice
  • The history of your stock
  • Your own sign-ins, which you can end

What do you record?

We record changes, with who made them and when.

Only RouteFlow staff can read that record. You can’t read it yet.

What don’t you have?

  • Two-step login
  • Company single sign-on
  • An API, or webhooks you set up
