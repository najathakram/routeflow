Questions before you start.

What is RouteFlow?

Software for businesses that sell to stores. Take orders, send invoices, and get paid.

Who is it for?

Wholesalers and distributors who sell to stores. We built it with a wholesaler who buys from other wholesalers and delivers to retailers.

How much does it cost?

Starter is $99 a month, Growth is $249, and Scale is $499. The plans differ by how many customers and logins they include. Scale and Enterprise also get regulated items at no fee. Pay for a year and get 2 months free.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every trial starts on Starter and runs for 14 days.

Can I bring my data?

Yes. Import customers, products, invoices, payments, and stock from CSV files. You can undo a product import for 24 hours, unless a product is already on an order.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Download your customers, products, orders, invoices, and payments as CSV files. Orders and invoices download as summaries, without line items, up to 1,000 rows at a time. See what you can download.

Does it connect to my other tools?

Not directly. RouteFlow doesn’t connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or Zoho. Move data with CSV files.

What doesn’t RouteFlow do?

It doesn’t keep stock in separate warehouses, keep a general ledger, or connect by EDI.

What do drivers and stores need?

Stores and drivers use a web browser on a phone or a computer. Drivers see the route and the stops, and can start or skip a stop.
