Product

What RouteFlow does.

RouteFlow is software for businesses that sell to stores. A store orders. You deliver it or hand it over. You send the bill. You get paid.

The main things you can do.

  • SellOrders, prices, promotions, and estimates.
  • Let stores orderStores see their own prices and order online.
  • Get paidBills, payments, and returns.
  • Buy and keep stockSuppliers and what you have.
  • DeliverRoutes and stops for drivers.
  • Sell at the counterScan items and print a receipt.
  • See your numbersSales, profit, and who owes you.
  • Sell regulated itemsGoods like tobacco, with extra legal rules.

How do orders reach your customers?

We ask this one question when you set up your account. Select one or more.

  • We deliver with our own trucks

    Select this, and routes and drivers are on, at no added fee.

  • We ship by carrier

    Select this, and an order can keep the carrier’s name. RouteFlow doesn’t connect to the carrier.

  • Customers pick up or buy at the counter

    Select this, and counter sales are on, at no fee.

To change it later, email us.

Who uses it.

There are four kinds of login. Each one reaches different things.

  • Admin

    Everything staff can do. Admins also change billing and settings, and add staff.

  • Staff

    Orders, customers, invoices, and stock.

  • Driver

    The route and the stops, in a web browser.

  • Store

    Its own prices, orders, and invoices, in a web browser.

Access goes by role. You can’t set it for one person.

How it works, and where it stops.

What happens to an order?

StepWhat happensThe rule
EstimateYou send it. It turns into an invoice.You can skip this step.
OrderStaff enter it, or the store orders online.Extra pieces roll into boxes. The price comes from the store’s level or its own price.
InvoiceIt starts as a draft, built from the order.It can’t be sent or edited until the order is delivered. Edit the order instead.
DeliveryIf you deliver, drivers start or skip each stop.An order on a run is delivered when its stop is completed. That unlocks its invoice.
PaymentYou record the amount and how it was paid.You see who has paid and who still owes you.
ReturnYou take goods back and give store credit.Cash back needs a fully paid invoice.

What can a driver do?

Can do

  • See the route and the stops
  • Open a stop in maps
  • Call the store
  • Start or skip a stop

Can’t do

  • Finish a stop
  • Take a photo or a signature
  • Take payment at the door

How is stock kept?

Each product has one count. Cost is a weighted average by default. You can switch to last cost.

A stock count can be paused and picked up on another device.

There’s no FIFO or LIFO, no lot numbers, no expiry dates, and no bins.

Where does it stop?

RouteFlow doesn’t:

  • Keep stock in separate warehouses
  • Keep a general ledger
  • Connect by EDI
  • Connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or Zoho
  • Connect to a carrier
  • Show drivers on a live map on the web
  • Send text messages. Notices go by email.

It’s the wrong choice if you need several warehouses, EDI customers, a general ledger, or lot and expiry tracking.

Where are the books?

Reports come from your sales and expenses. RouteFlow also tracks expenses, receipts, and mileage.

There’s no general ledger, chart of accounts, journals, balance sheet, or period close.

Keep your accountant and your accounting tool. Move data with CSV files.

How is access checked?

Both the screen and the server check the login’s role.

Admin covers everything staff can do. A driver or store login has its own, smaller set.

  • You sell by the box and by the piece.
  • You deliver with your own trucks, sell at a counter, or both.
