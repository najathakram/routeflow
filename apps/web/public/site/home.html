Use cases

What {{brand:name}} helps you do.

Every trial includes all of these.

  • Take orders

    A store calls, orders online, or orders what it bought last week.

  • Sell by box or piece

    Take orders in boxes, pieces, or both.

  • Set each store’s price

    Set a price for one store or a group of stores. Every order uses it.

  • See the last price

    See what a store paid last time. Use that price again.

  • Let stores order online

    Stores see their own prices and order online.

  • Let stores see their account

    Stores see their invoices, their payments, and where each order is.

  • Send invoices and get paid

    Turn the order into an invoice. Send it after delivery. Record the check. You see who has paid and who still owes you.

  • See who’s late

    See what’s owed by days late: 1 to 15, 16 to 30, 31 to 45, or more than 45.

  • Send statements

    Email a store its statement, or save it as a PDF.

  • Send a reminder

    Remind a store about an invoice it hasn’t paid.

  • Follow every check

    Recorded, deposited, cleared, or bounced. If a check bounces, the invoice opens again.

  • Part and early payments

    Record part of an invoice, one payment across invoices, or money paid early.

  • Add sales tax

    Set your tax rate. Orders add it. Tax-exempt stores pay none, and you keep their certificates.

  • Send estimates and repeat invoices

    Send an estimate first. Repeat an invoice on a schedule.

  • Take returns

Questions before you start.

What is {{brand:name}} for?

Software for businesses that sell to stores. Take orders, send invoices, and get paid.

Who is it for?

Wholesalers and distributors who sell to stores. We built it with a wholesaler who buys from other wholesalers and delivers to retailers.

How much does it cost?

Starter is $99 a month, Growth is $249, and Scale is $499. The plans differ by how many customers they include. Scale and Enterprise also get regulated items at no fee. Pay for a year and get 2 months free.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every trial starts on Starter and runs for 14 days.

Can I bring my data?

Yes. Import customers, products, invoices, payments, and stock from CSV files. You can undo a product import for 24 hours, unless a product is already on an order.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Download your customers, products, orders, invoices, and payments as CSV files. Orders and invoices download as summaries, without line items, up to 1,000 rows at a time. See what you can download.

Does it connect to my other tools?

Not directly. {{brand:name}} doesn’t connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or Zoho. Move data with CSV files.

What doesn’t {{brand:name}} do?

It doesn’t keep stock in separate warehouses, keep a general ledger, or connect by EDI.

What do drivers and stores need?

Stores and drivers use a web browser on a phone or a computer. Drivers see the route and the stops, and can start or skip a stop.
