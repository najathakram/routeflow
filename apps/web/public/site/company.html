Why we built it

We built this with a wholesaler, for wholesalers.

If you’ve tried software for your business and given up, I understand why.

You sell one product by the box and by the piece. Every store wants something different. Your day is already full, and not everyone who works with you is good with technology. Software has to fit all of that, or it goes unused.

My co-founder’s father is a wholesaler. He buys from other wholesalers and delivers to stores. He tried several apps and services, and none of them fit the way he works. So he asked us for help, and we built RouteFlow with him. Then his friends tried it, and we made it a product so it could help you too.

RouteFlow runs your selling, from order to payment. You take the order, send the bill, and see who paid. Stores can order for themselves, each at its own price. It also covers buying, simple stock, and delivery routes.

Many apps list the same features. We work to make ours hold up in daily use. When a line has 2 boxes and 3 pieces, the bill adds up right.

I want to be honest with you. RouteFlow is still new. It has most of what a wholesaler needs, but not everything, and we list what it does and doesn’t do so you can check before you start. If you need something it doesn’t do, tell us. We can customize it for your business, and we’ll tell you what that takes and what it costs.

You move your data in yourself, with CSV files, and our first customers get setup help from us by email. Software like this can cost more than a small business can pay, so we show the prices for Starter, Growth, and Scale before you start.

Try it with your own orders for 14 days, with no card. If it doesn’t fit the way you work, tell us at hello@routeflow.info. We reply within one business day.
