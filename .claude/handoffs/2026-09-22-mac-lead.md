#### ⚠️ RUNBOOK CORRECTION — killing the jest tree is NOT enough

Killing the jest workers **does not stop the run.** Turbo's
`--continue=dependencies-successful` moves to the next task and **re-spawns fresh
PIDs within seconds** (observed: `test:repo-truth` + a `bugs-self-test` prover).
A half-kill *looks* like it worked — pressure drops — and then ramps straight back up.

**Kill the WHOLE tree, scoped to the worktree path:**

```bash
pkill -f rf-lane-<name>            # catches jest, turbo, npm parents together
```

Then verify `pgrep -f <worktree>` returns **0**, not just that pressure fell.
Jest is read-only, so commits are never at risk from the kill.

task from an env var, defaulting to today's exact behaviour so CI is bit-for-bit
unchanged. **Built and pushed from the PC** (31.5 GB, no Photoshop) — which dissolves
