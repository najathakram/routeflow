# Customer notice: RouteFlow becomes Rovio

Four texts: a heads-up at D0 and a launch-day note, each for the 14-day and the 7-day plan. Pick one plan. You need only the two texts for it.

## Rules for sending

- Do not send anything until the trademark attorney has cleared the name in writing. The notice names the new brand, so it is public.
- Notice 1 goes out at D0. Launch day is 14 days later (plan A) or 7 days later (plan B). Notice 2 goes out on launch day.
- Replace `[launch date]` with the real date, for example "December 8".
- If the name changes to the fallback (XROV.IO), swap only the name. The web address and email address stay the same.

## What the notice says, and where it comes from

- **Changes:** the name, the web address (www.xrovio.com), the email sender name and our contact address (hello@xrovio.com).
- **Does not change:** logins, data, plan and price, invoice numbers, how you order.
- **Also true:** the old web address and old email address keep working. Links in old emails keep working. You may need to sign in again on the new address. Some screens may still show the old name for a while.
- **Left out on purpose:** the card statement name, the mobile app name in the store, and the date every screen shows the new name. None has a date yet, so the notice does not promise one.

Source: HANDOFF section 2 (host, email, prices, one launch day), spec delta section 1 and 2 (old mailboxes at least 12 months, old links, one new sign-in per address, the old address keeps the app), email spec (sender name from the brand), program outline for the app (what changes later).

# Plan A: 14 days' notice

## A1. Notice 1 (at D0, 14 days before launch)

**Subject:** RouteFlow becomes Rovio on [launch date]

**Preheader:** Your logins, data and prices stay the same.

**Body:**

Hello,

On [launch date], RouteFlow becomes Rovio.

What changes:
- The name. You will see Rovio on our website and in our emails.
- The web address: www.xrovio.com.
- Our contact address: hello@xrovio.com.

What does not change:
- Your logins and your data.
- Your plan and your price.
- Your invoice numbers.
- How you order.

The old web address and the old email address keep working. Links in old emails keep working too. You may need to sign in again on the new address. Some screens may show the old name for a while.

Questions? Write to hello@routeflow.info.

**In-app banner:** RouteFlow becomes Rovio on [launch date]. Your logins and data stay the same.

**Subject:** RouteFlow is now Rovio

**Preheader:** New name and web address. Everything else stays the same.

**Body:**

## Checks before you send

- Banner lengths: A1 77, A2 59, B1 80, B2 59 characters (limit 90).
- Each body is 100 to 106 words (limit 150).
- "Your invoice numbers" has no written source in the plan. The rebrand touches no invoice data, but the lead should confirm it before the notice goes out.
- The 14-day plan matches the checklist wording "customers told two weeks ago and today". The 7-day plan needs your yes.
