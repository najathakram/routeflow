# Wording sitting: 53 proposed text changes

Read this on your phone, one block at a time. Each block shows the current text, the proposed text, why, and my advice.

## Summary

- The ledger holds 53 proposed changes. That matches the number in the hand-over. I counted them row by row.
- 8 of them repeat another change (the same closing line on 6 pages counts as 5 repeats, plus 2 wheel lines that match home, plus the dimmed Send text on the product page). So there are 45 different decisions.
- I recommend accepting 52 as they are.
- 1 needs a different text: block 32 (the "this screen" line on the data page).
- All proposals keep the name RouteFlow. The rename is a separate step.
- 14 other questions in the ledger are not in these 53. They need your answer before anyone writes text. The ones that touch these blocks: "bill" or "invoice" (blocks 7 and 17), "store" or "buyer" (blocks 11, 21 and 42), one product description (block 53).

### The 5 that most need your eye

1. Block 18: removes the full founder letter from the home page. The company page keeps it, and home keeps a short summary with a link.
2. Blocks 19 and 29: cut "I want to be honest with you." from the founder letter. It is your voice, so you decide.
3. Blocks 4 and 5: delete "Includes your team." and "Need more than Scale? Email us about Enterprise." on the pricing page. The second one changes how a big buyer reaches you. The first touches the open question on how many logins a plan includes.
4. Blocks 7 and 17: use "repeat invoices" everywhere instead of "recurring invoices". Home also loses "Your team" from the plan list. One name for the feature across the site.
5. Block 53: the one-line description shown when someone shares a link. It becomes "Software for businesses that sell to stores."
