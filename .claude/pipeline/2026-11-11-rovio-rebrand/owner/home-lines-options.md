# Home lines: three sets to choose from (owner box, item 1 of SA-1174)

X is the new name. The owner asked for lines that sound like a person talking, not a slogan.

Every line keeps the name away from the very end, so the fallback name never reads "XROV.IO." with two dots.

## Recommended: set 1

| Where | Today | Set 1 | Set 2 | Set 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wheel line and home sub-line | Every step is in X. | You do every step in X, start to finish. | X keeps it all together, from the order to the payment. | You do all of it in X, in one place. |
| Section heading | What you can do with X. | What X helps you do. | Here's what X does, step by step. | What you'll use X for. |
| FAQ question | What is X? | What is X for? | What is X, in plain words? | What is X, and who is it for? |

- **Set 1** is the plainest. Each line is short and easy for someone reading in their second language.
- **Set 2** sounds most like a friend showing you around.
- **Set 3** has the FAQ that best fits the answer under it ("Software for businesses that sell to stores...").

## What stays the same

- On the home page the wheel line follows "Buy, sell, get paid. Then buy again." Each set's line reads well there and on its own.
- The FAQ answer does not change.
- The Pricing FAQ keeps its own question unless you want the two pages to match.
- The line must be the same in both places it appears: the home page (`home.html:114`) and the wheel script (`rf-wheel-up.js:47`).

## Not used

- "X keeps every step in one place." / "What X does for you." / "What does X do?" (rejected 2026-11-19 ~20:22Z: too much like a slogan).
- "Every step is in X, start to finish." (the earlier alternative; it still ends near the name and reads like a tagline).
