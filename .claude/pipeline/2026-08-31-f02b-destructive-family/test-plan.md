`tenantTransaction` as a pass-through, so a spec that "proves" tenant scoping against it proves
nothing. Every tenancy assertion below uses either (a) a fake store that models tenant
partitioning and drives the REAL `_wrapTxWithTenant`/`forTenant` (the
`settings.controller.clear-financial.spec.ts` shape from #506), or (b) argument-shape assertions
on the exact `where` clauses — never the pass-through mock alone.
