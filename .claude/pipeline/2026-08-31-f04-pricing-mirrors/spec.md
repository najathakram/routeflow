## Deploy-day answer

Pure-function changes; no schema, no gate, no flag. Deploy-day effect is immediate and wholly
beneficial: promo selection stops over-billing mixed boxed lines, rounding matches the audit
recomputation, driver at-door totals match invoices. One measurable behavior change to disclose:
lines where the OLD selection picked the wrong promo will now bill less — that is the fix, and
the register documents the overcharge. No backfill (historical invoices are untouched — the
repair-lane question is the campaign's exit-criterion-4 decision, not this batch's).

## Non-goals (fenced)

- `invoices.service.ts` untouched (F03's lane). The server telescope is the reference oracle.
- No web call-site adoption of `prorateLineSubtotal` (exported only; F05+ wire consumers).
- No consolidation of the four files into one package (the mirrors-by-policy structure stays).
- No change to `promoBogoFreeUnits` semantics or promo eligibility (`applyBestPromotion`'s
  selection basis only).
