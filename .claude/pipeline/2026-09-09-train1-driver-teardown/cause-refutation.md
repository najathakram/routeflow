## Risks for the fix design

1. **Ordering.** `auth-store.ts:68` awaits `apiLogout()` **first**, and `auth.ts:283-293` deletes
   every token inside it. Anything needing a token (a final queue flush, a "stop tracking" ping)
   must run _before_ that call. GPS teardown should also precede it — after token deletion the
   background task keeps sampling and its `postLocation` 401s silently
   (`location-tracker.native.ts:20-25`).
2. **Reachability.** `queryClient` is not exported (`_layout.tsx:38`); B140 needs a module-level
   singleton or a registration hook (`registerStaffSessionExpiredHandler`, `api-client.ts:95-97`,
   is the in-repo pattern).
3. **Device-scoped keys.** Clearing `tenantSlug` on logout also wipes the company code, so the
   next sign-in on a shared dispatch tablet re-enters it and loses the branded login screen
   (`api-client.ts:88-92`'s comment explicitly relies on the slug surviving a token wipe).
4. **Migration.** Stamping identity onto `QueuedAction` must tolerate already-persisted
   AsyncStorage rows with no such field (treat `undefined` as "legacy — belongs to nobody").

## Open product questions for the Lead (3)

1. **Sign out while offline with queued work** — drop the queue, or keep it and replay only when
   the _same_ user signs back in (identity-stamped, mismatched entries surfaced as
   `failedActions` rather than executed)? Same question for the persisted `failedActions` list,
   which today shows driver A's failures to driver B.
2. **Tenant slug on logout** — clear it (clean multi-tenant handover) or keep it (shared-tablet
   convenience, branded login)? These conflict; `tenant-store.clear()` exists for the former.
3. **POD durability scope** — persist `podStore` only, or also `runSettlementStore` (driver cash
   tally, same in-memory shape, money-adjacent)? And on relaunch after a kill, does the driver
   re-capture (accepting the duplicate-photo append at `routes.service.ts:2122`) or does the app
   reconcile against the stop's existing `podPhotoUrls` first?
