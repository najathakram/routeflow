*(Group G, 4 RUN-LOG cites, 8 days, 4 distinct runs)*

plan-created path is deferred and re-proven after implement wave 1, not classed broken; (c) plan
templates default full-suite Jest to `--maxWorkers=2`, `--reporters=default` last,
`--passWithNoTests` on any final command targeting only new specs.

Why now: the only unactioned 4-cite group; one cited run had **zero final gate coverage**, a
quality-floor breach, not an efficiency question. The ad-hoc `--passWithNoTests` fix at
train4-damage-report proves the cost is cheap and it still isn't a standing rule.

## 5. Learning-loop health (one observation)

The ledger's own escalation counter fired **0 times across 43 runs**, while RUN-LOG prose alone
holds one group at 5 cites and two at 3-4 — recurrence currently lives only in text the ledger
never reads, which is why this first retro landed at 43 runs / 11 true-telemetry rows instead of
catching Group H ten days ago. On the input side: **4 light-loop entries are bare `Knob candidate:
TODO` stubs** ("no confirmedByPhase data in result.json") that nothing ever revisited — light-loop
signal is lost at the point of capture. Add the 8 unrecorded red-gate rows and the single-quoted
4/4 siblingSweep incident, and the shape repeats: the record step accepts an empty record. Two
mechanical fixes, both cheaper than P1/P2: (a) closeout refuses a `TODO` stub — require one real
sentence or an explicit `none — light loop, no phases`; (b) the ledger's escalation trigger should
count RUN-LOG group recurrence, not only in-engine phase triggers. Until then every retro is manual
and the 3x rule has no alarm.
