## #3694 `924eebf8ee` Bookkeeping windows on the tenant's own day (SA-906, SA-1057, W-728)

- `common/calendar-date.ts`: new `PeriodRange` (`{ fromDay?, toDay? }` | `"today" | "week" | "month" | "quarter" | "year"` | `{ month: number }`), `TenantPeriod` ({ fromDay, toDay, `days`, `instants` }) and `tenantPeriod(range, zone, now?)`, the ONE period every Bookkeeping and analytics report windows on. Two column classes, never mixed: `days` (`{ gte, lt }` UTC-midnight stamps, no zone shift) for calendar-date columns (`Invoice.issueDate`, `dueDate`, `Expense.date`); `instants` (the tenant's own days, built on `localInstantWindow`) for real-instant columns (`CreditNote.createdAt`, `Return.refundedAt`, `Invoice.writtenOffAt`, `StockMovement.effectiveAt`). Kinds are relative to `tenantTodayDay(zone, now)`; week starts Sunday; a null or unknown zone is UTC. Also new `calendarDayRange(from?, to?)` (open-ended `{ gte?, lt? }` for list endpoints, `lt` = the day after `to`; replaces a hand-built `setUTCHours(23, 59, 59, 999)`).
- `common/invoiced-sales.ts`: new `eventWindowOf(window, timeZone?, now?)` (instant filter for credit notes, external refunds and write-offs from a report's day window; the all-time year-9999 sentinel ends now). The accrual net-sales readers and `fetchBadDebtExpense` take `opts.timeZone` and filter credits, refunds and write-offs through it (the inline copy in the by-period reader is replaced by the helper).
- `bookkeeping/bookkeeping.service.ts`: every report and the dashboard (today, week, month, quarter, year and the monthly buckets) resolve the zone once (`tenantTimeZone(this.prisma)`, UTC fallback) and window through `tenantPeriod`; private `reportPeriod`, `windowOf`, `receiptsWindow` (receipts stay on the UTC day: a mixed column, deferred by SA-906, as are the regulated period bucket and `CounterDayClose`). Release effect: a credit issued late on a month's last evening now belongs to that month.
- New `apps/api/scripts/report-bookkeeping-month-shift-census.mjs`: read-only census (`BEGIN READ ONLY`, timeouts, closed in `finally`; `--months N`, `--self-test`) of events that change month between the UTC and tenant calendars; `STOP_DOLLARS = 500` per tenant-month marks STOP; prints internal tenant ids only. The owner runs it on prod BEFORE the merge, by contract. Registered in `common/scripts-live-target-guard.spec.ts`.
- Tests: `common/calendar-date.tenant-period.spec`, `invoices/paid-window-bounds.tenant-period.spec`, `bookkeeping/bookkeeping.tenant-day.pin.spec`, `analytics/analytics-tenant-day.db.spec` (DB lane, real Postgres), `common/report-bookkeeping-month-shift-census.spec`, `bookkeeping.service.spec` extended.
