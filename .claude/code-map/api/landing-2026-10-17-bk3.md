## B846 bill revert appends; PO edit reversal is a PURCHASE (#3496, #3492)

- `inventory/bill-revert.ts` (NEW) pairing rules in one place: `billReceiptNote(billNumber)`, `billReversalNote(originalId, filedThrough?)`, `billReversalTargetId(notes)`, `poReversalNote`, `poReversalOf({type, quantity, notes})` (negative PURCHASE, or legacy ADJUSTMENT), `pairedReceiptIds(db, productId)`.
- `inventory/stock-write-date.ts` (NEW) one filed-period predicate: `documentStockDate(db, {tenantId, productIds, documentDate, now}) -> Map<id, {effectiveAt, movedFromPeriod}>`. A date inside a period filed through (`TobaccoReport`, `RegulatedFiling` GENERATED/LOCKED) moves forward to now; if now is filed too, 409 `STOCK_DATE_IN_FILED_PERIOD`. No tenant: throws.
- `vendor-bills/vendor-bills.service.ts` `revertToDraft(id, performedById?)` deletes nothing: `loadLiveBillReceipts` matches unpaired positive PURCHASE rows PER PRODUCT (reference, exact receipt note) and refuses with 409 `INVENTORY_LEDGER_GAP` ("void and re-enter") when their sum differs from the lines.
- It appends one negative PURCHASE per receipt row, dated at the receipt or now when filed, replays a product when newer rows exist (`gapsDetected`), under `retryStockTx`. `vendor-bills.controller.ts` `revert-to-draft` passes the user id. `receive` stamps `billReceiptNote` and dates non-PO bills through `documentStockDate`.
- `reverseBillLots(tx, billNumber, receipts?, billDate?)`: each receipt's lot is matched by full tuple; a miss falls back to bill-date (legacy) lots and reverses only when candidates do not outnumber receipts, else `INVENTORY_LEDGER_GAP`. Untouched lots are deleted, consumed ones zeroed.
- Readers skip both halves of a reversed pair: `inventory/sale-movement.ts` `resolveSaleCost` (LAST_COST = last positive unpaired PURCHASE) and `analytics/analytics.service.ts` `getCostHistory` (positive unpaired PURCHASE plus COST_BASIS).
- `inventory.service.ts` `recomputeProductInTx` treats a negative costed PURCHASE as the exact inverse (`reverseAverageCost`), never `nextAverageCost`.
- PO edit: `reversePoReceipts` writes a negative PURCHASE (was ADJUSTMENT) per live receipt at the receipt's date or now when filed, returns `{lotDates, receipts, earliestAt, ownRowIds}`. `updatePurchaseOrder` re-posts one PURCHASE per reversed receipt (oldest first) in its own month, ONE lot per line, replays products with newer foreign rows, returns `gapsDetected`.
- `postPoReceipt(..., effectiveAt?, lotQty?: Decimal | false) -> movementId | null`. Error code `STOCK_DATE_IN_FILED_PERIOD` (409) in both error-code files.
