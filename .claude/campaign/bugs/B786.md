## Root cause

From SA-107 (2026-09-23, the #1293/B774 read); fleet lead filing request. Verified on origin/master 2ed80789: fetchMatchingSet resolves a candidate's pack as `item.unitsPerBox ?? productUpbMap.get(item.productId) ?? null` (:342; :370 on #1293's head 149f73be) — a line with a NULL pack snapshot silently takes the LIVE product pack, with no warn and no counter. SA-107 fixes the BOX-AWARE case in #1293 (packFromOwnSplit first, so the line's own split wins); this record is the remaining BOX-UNAWARE null-snapshot case, which has no split to derive from. Durable fix: B774's follow-up — carry orderItemId on the wire so the quote prices the exact line. Until then: add a warn and a counter on the live-pack fallback. Owner: Lane IR, with the B774 orderItemId follow-up.
