## Reported evidence

At the single-column breakpoint (max-width:950px), .booking-copy/.booking-panel are grid items of .booking-layout that default to min-width:auto and refuse to shrink below their own content's min-content width, pinning them ~6px wider than the viewport at 390px — a small horizontal-scroll overflow (scrollWidth 396 vs innerWidth 390), invisible in a screenshot but felt as sideways rubber-banding on scroll. Found via a production 390/768/1440 viewport sweep across all 11 marketing routes (2026-09-18).
