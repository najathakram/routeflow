## Reported evidence

From the lead (reproduced on a clean master worktree on the PC). apps/control's jest suites fail on Windows: origin.spec expects file mode 0o600 and gets 0o666. A full-scope pre-push, which is forced when a change touches .husky/ or .github/, therefore can NEVER pass on the PC, which blocks PC lanes from pushing tooling or CI changes without SKIP_VERIFY. Two finished superseding branches (#2735 v2, #2484 v2) are parked locally on the PC until it is fixed. Fix: make the mode assertions platform-aware (skip on win32 with a reason) and audit the other control specs. Tooling, medium.
