## Root cause

A customer advance can be spent twice — the wallet debit takes no lock and the balance may go negative

Symptom / evidence: Two overlapping applies of a $100 advance mark $200 of invoices paid and leave AdvancePayment.balance at -$100; the same path also flips a DRAFT invoice to PAID and counts unconfirmed DRAFT payments.

Files implicated: apps/api/src/customers/customers.service.ts, apps/api/src/credit-notes/credit-notes.service.ts, apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts, apps/mobile/app/(operator)/(tabs)/invoices/[id].tsx.
