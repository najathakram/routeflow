## Root cause

The buyer/online anti-overpay guard names row locks that are never taken, so a concurrent office payment still overpays

Symptom / evidence: A buyer cash approval and an operator payment on the same invoice both read the pre-payment balance, both pass the balance check, and the invoice ends up paid beyond its total.

Files implicated: apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts, apps/api/src/payment-requests/payment-requests.service.ts, apps/api/src/prisma/prisma.service.ts.
