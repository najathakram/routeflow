## Reported evidence

invoices.service.ts update() re-derives a stored box split at the product's LIVE unitsPerBox. packFromOwnSplit exists for exactly this case ('never from the live product, which may have been repacked since') and has one caller in the whole repo, estimates.service.ts:472; siblings at invoices.service.ts:2334 and :826-836 already use it -- update() was missed. Fires on ANY invoice save after a unitsPerBox edit: products.service.ts:865-880 allows free pack edits with no draft-invoice check; the web sends boxes/pieces beside qty and normalizeBoxesPieces ignores qty when a split is present; the three upstream guards check unitLabel, which a pack change does not carry; update() calls assertInvoiceRewriteAllowed (unit-aware-guard.ts:143-175) which does not catch this shape either. The halved qty/invoicedQty back-syncs onto the ORDER line at :2346-2355, so the corruption escapes the invoice entirely. Fix: route update() through packFromOwnSplit like its siblings. Server-only surface. Fast-follow -- part of the live-pack-reinterpreting-a-stored-line class.
