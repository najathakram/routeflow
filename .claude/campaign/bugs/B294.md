## Root cause

Order-derived and estimate-converted invoice lines carry taxRate 0, so any later recompute silently drops the sales tax to $0

Symptom / evidence: A $100 order at 7.5% bills $107.50; a price adjustment to $95 rewrites tax to $0.00 and total to $95.00 instead of $102.13, while the order says $102.13.
