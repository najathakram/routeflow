## Fix approach and UX

The source-numbered update branch must refuse to update an invoice whose number was minted by the importer's fallback (external ref source import present without a source number on that invoice) - treat it as a new invoice plus a warning row instead of silently updating the fallback-minted one.

## Reported evidence

The fallback branch mints INV-<year>-#### for source-numberless rows via NumberingService.reserveNext. A later upload whose own Invoice Number column happens to equal one of those minted numbers takes the source-numbered branch instead and UPDATES that unrelated live invoice (status/dueDate/paidAt), reporting it as updated - the collision guard only proves the candidate free at mint time, not against a future upload's source number. Money-sensitive: silently mutates a live invoice's status and paid state. Found by the Group A final pass (numbering siblings B267/B268/B269).
