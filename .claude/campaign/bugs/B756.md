## Root cause

Found by the Opus review of #1272 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); verified on origin/master 2ed80789: the legacy 'no stop has linked orders' fallback's order.findMany filters status notIn [CANCELLED] only (:1309), with no take/limit, so DELIVERED history for those customers is fetched and shown; after #1273 those orders are also quoted with tax. getRunPackingList (:2739) already excludes both: notIn [CANCELLED, DELIVERED]. Fix: exclude DELIVERED the same way (and bound the query); the durable fix remains retiring the fallback, per its own F11 comment.
