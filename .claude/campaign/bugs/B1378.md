## Reported evidence

W-1316 (P1). A counter return can issue a credit larger than the amount the customer actually paid on the sale. Owner ruling 2026-11-23, clarified about 18:10Z: a returned item is never credited above the price the customer was charged for it, whatever the sale payment state (the earlier wording, what was paid, is superseded). Never-sold items stay at a staff-set price, default today (W-1316 PR-2 is separate, not this bug). SA-1273 read three paths, all capped by #4462: matched returns price at the invoice line (SA-840); an unreferenced chunk of a sold-before item is capped at the last charged price per selling unit; a staff override is capped at that default (400 RETURN_CREDIT_OVER_CAP). The counter goes through the same quote and capture. Money and ledger path; HIGH-risk.
