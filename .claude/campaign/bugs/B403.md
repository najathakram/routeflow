## Reported evidence

#725 fixed taxRate storage going forward, but pre-fix invoices keep taxRate=0 on order-derived lines: (a) an adjustment on any invoice created before #725 still zeroes regular tax (the fix is forward-only), and (b) tobacco/excise reports compute tax collected as subtotal*taxRate and will silently mix 0-rate pre-fix rows with real post-fix rows inside one reporting period. Needs an owner-signed backfill (rate derivable as (invoice.taxAmount - sum(categoryTaxAmount)) / invoice.subtotal, or from the linked order) plus a report-side note. No client identifiers.
