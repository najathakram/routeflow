## Reported evidence

Codes sharing a prefix (e.g. 200000000001 / 200000000002) can both resolve to the same wrong product and merge into one line; codes held only in the barcode table are not found. Should resolve via resolveProductByCode / pickBestScanMatch with a picker on ambiguity; a failed lookup must not read as No product found. Found by the lead's hunt of the counter-sale scan path.
