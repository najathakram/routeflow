# B754 · Returns: mixed-unit returns are refused (B650 interim); a proper unit-aware conversion should read each line's quantity in its own unit and accept them

## Root cause

Follow-up to B650 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23). B650's fix (Lane IR, 939ec6e1, unpushed at filing) REFUSES a STANDARD return when a product's non-cancelled lines on the order are in more than one unit (a pack/case line plus a units_v1 unitLabel line), because soldQtyForProduct sums raw qty across units and the result is denominated in nothing real. This follow-up replaces the refusal with a proper conversion: read each line's typed quantity in its OWN unit (unitLabel / factor, or boxes/pieces for a legacy pack line), express sold and returned in base pieces, and accept the mixed-unit return. Medium, NOT units-gating: the refusal is the safe interim.
