## Reported evidence

SA-077(2)'s unpackable-split refusal (the fix that prevents an ambiguous boxes/pieces split from being silently accepted) lands on the invoice endpoints, which are operator-only by role and by call graph. The buyer order endpoints DO accept boxes and pieces, unlike invoices, so they are the surface where a staff-reason-versus-buyer-generic message distinction would actually fire in practice -- confirmed by the architect as what the message rule was written for. Follow-up to SA-077(2), not part of it: same predicate applied at this different, buyer-reachable surface, with the generic (non-staff-reason) message variant. Fast-follow.
