## Reported evidence

From the lead (separate Opus refute-first pass; evidence in the lead's hunt-conservation notes). settleStockForEdit never applies deliveredQty on the increase side and its decrease cap is pooled per product rather than per line. Repros: stock 100 and a line of 10 delivered, edit to 6 then 10 gives 86 (true 90); 4 of 10 delivered, 10 to 2 to 10 gives 88; pooled: removing a delivered line A (5 of 5) while B (5, undelivered) stays gives 95. Fix: a per-line clamp on both sides (effHeld = max(held, delivered), effReq = max(req, delivered), matched by line id). Stock, tier 1, api, high. Lane: PC builder G, after live stock.
