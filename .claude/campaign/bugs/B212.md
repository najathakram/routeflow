## Reported evidence

reopenStop (routes.service.ts ~:2695-2697) accepts a SKIPPED stop and would flip a COMPLETED run back to IN_PROGRESS, but POST /route-runs/:id/stops/:stopId/reopen has exactly one client repo-wide — useReopenStop (apps/mobile/lib/api/routes.ts ~:674-683) — whose only caller renders the control solely for s.status === COMPLETED; a SKIPPED stop falls to the null branch, and apps/web has no stop-reopen caller at all. So B211's stranded orders have no in-product recovery and B146's 'only until a human intervenes' was false. Found by the F11 analysis (Opus A, 2026-09-02).
