# B1385 · A DRAFT payment's leftover was treated as spendable customer credit (SA-1281 part 1, F6)

**Location** `apps/api/src/customers/available-credit.ts (isAdvanceAvailable and its readers); apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts standalone payment writer` · **Severity** high · **Batch** F65 · **State** already-fixed

## Summary

_Not yet analysed._

## What this feature is for

_Not yet analysed._

## Root cause

Unconfirmed money (a DRAFT payment) was read as credit by three readers that each decided availability on their own. The advance row of a null-source draft passed isAdvanceAvailable.

## User impact

_Not yet analysed._

## Fix approach and UX

One filter, filterAvailableAdvances, serves the apply list, the apply write and the Payments tile; the standalone writer refuses a DRAFT with a leftover (DRAFT_PAYMENT_HAS_EXCESS) and a standalone allocation onto a DRAFT invoice (STRIPE_SETTLE exempt). Fixed by #4512.

## Test plan

T1. available-credit.draft-advance.spec.ts, available-credit.sites.spec.ts (inventory pin: every advance reader classified), payment-list-advance-tile.spec.ts, invoices.service.draft-payment.spec.ts. Old null-source draft advances stay until the owner's read-only count.

## Reported evidence

Recording a DRAFT payment of 500 with no allocation returned 201 and the customer's advance showed 500, because the advance row has no source payment and isAdvanceAvailable called an unsourced advance spendable. A DRAFT group's 200 excess could be applied to a SENT invoice and landed as a PAID ADVANCE row, and the apply list and the Payments tile summed it. A staff allocation onto a DRAFT invoice wrote a PAID row on an invoice that stayed DRAFT.
