## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Two items scanned during one slow catalog lookup both land in the cart.

**Actually does.** While a resolve was in flight, ScanCamera's busy flag dropped every new detection on the floor — after the success haptic had already fired. Fast scanning against a slow network produced carts missing lines with no indication anything was lost.

**The gap.** Busy-drop instead of a queue: the accept decision (the gate's job) was being silently overruled downstream.
