## Reported evidence

reverseReturnEntries over-reverses when the returned quantity and the SALE row's qty are in different units. On a box-unaware regulated line (qty 6 = 6 BOXES) a return of 12 pieces closes the row (closesRow = remaining >= rowRemaining, :331) and books the WHOLE remaining net and tax (:344-345): 100% of net sales and category tax reversed instead of 16.7%; a 3-piece return reverses 50% instead of 4.2%. UNTRACED LINK (the lead: nobody fixes B660 until someone traces that caller): the Mac Lead traced ONE hop, returns.service.ts receive() (~495-510) builds returnedByProduct as the sum of Number(item.qty) of the ReturnItems per product and passes it to reverseReturnEntries UNCONVERTED. NOT TRACED BY ANYONE: what unit ReturnItem.qty is in when it is created. That it is typed and stored in PIECES rests on a sweeper claim plus returns-pieces.util.ts:1-11 ('OrderItem.qty is ALWAYS stored as total PIECES'), which is false for box-unaware lines (see B647 and B658 for how a boxed product is stored boxes null with qty counting boxes). The mechanism is sound IF that premise holds; until the create() side is traced this is unconfirmed. HOW KNOWN: R-ui verified the arithmetic; the receive() hop was traced by the Mac Lead; the rest is NOT traced; NEVER RUN. Class: invisible and accruing (regulated filing source, over-reversed net sales and category tax). NO LEGAL CONCLUSION is drawn: a compliance question for the owner's advisor, paired with the ledger soldAt question (not an id). Severity HIGH as ruled by the lead.
