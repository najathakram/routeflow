## Reported evidence

The line price is tap-to-edit (LineItemRow.tsx:203-210, one div with onClick/role=button) but carries no visible affordance -- only cursor-pointer, which has no effect on touch -- so operators on a phone (owner-reported, iPhone, in production) cannot discover that the price can be changed. Wiring to the PriceEditor is intact and correct (CreateOrderModal.tsx:1207-1218, :2064-2065; ScanOrderScreen.tsx:388-390, :407) -- this is purely a discoverability gap, no behaviour change needed. Fix: add a visible affordance (icon + aria-label + helper text at phone widths) when onTapPrice is set. R-ui builds it tonight after B700. Fast-follow, UI-only.
