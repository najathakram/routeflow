## Reported evidence

From the lead (separate Opus refute-first pass; details in the lead's local hunt notes). The merge tx never re-checks eligibility and deletes losers by id only. Cancel, invoice and route assignment do not take the merge lock, and the invoice foreign keys are SET NULL, so an orphan invoice can be billed twice. Fix: lock the orders FOR UPDATE in id order, re-select with the eligibility filter inside the tx, and make the delete conditional with a count check. Money, tier 1, api, high. Lane: Mac.
