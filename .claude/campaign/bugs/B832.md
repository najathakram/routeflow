# B832 · A VIEWED deposit mirror is not PAYABLE, so a door payment creates a fresh DRAFT beside it instead of restating the mirror

## Root cause

From the #1319 Opus delta (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23). Verified on origin/master b6e6bc7f: reconcileOrderDraftInvoice treats a deposit mirror in SENT/VIEWED/PARTIAL/OVERDUE as the order's invoice (:1361, :1389), but PAYABLE (invoice-status-sets.ts:24, used by recordDeliveryPaymentInTx and delivered-reconcile.ts:52) is DRAFT/SENT/PARTIAL/OVERDUE, without VIEWED. So once a customer opens the mirror (VIEWED), it drops out of payableInvoices, and at the door the server creates a fresh DRAFT next to the mirror instead of restating it: two open invoices for one order. SA ruling needed: does VIEWED belong in PAYABLE (issued-and-unpaid like SENT), or does the mirror set drop VIEWED? Pin whichever way with a two-set parity spec. Owner: unowned.
