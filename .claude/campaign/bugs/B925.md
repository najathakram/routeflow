## Reported evidence

One pg_advisory_xact_lock per barcode code is held for the whole catalog in a single transaction. "out of shared memory" at max_locks_per_transaction x connections (6,400 at the defaults) fails the call and can fail other tenants transactions. After #2860 fold (b) the >1,000-code cap refuses it; this ticket makes large clears possible. Fix: a chunked clearAll with at most 200 products per tx, each with its own sorted code-lock set, idempotent on re-run (inactive products skipped), reporting progress; then lift the cap. Pin: a real-Postgres clear of more than 1,000 referenced products completes with no lock-table error. Source: SA-603l (#2554 5919506098).
