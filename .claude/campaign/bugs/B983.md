## Reported evidence

From the B964-2 plan review (#2554), via the lead. Category and subcategory NAMES are user-typed and are not formula-guarded at their writer, and tracked-categories.service.ts (~:412) mirrors a subcategory name into Product.category, so a formula-led name can reach the verbatim regulated/tobacco filings through the product category. Fix: apply the TEXT_CELL_FORMULA_LEAD guard at the category-name writer (create and rename), change-only (existing rows untouched), with a coded 400. API, low (verbatim-filing exposure).
