## Root cause

From SA-104a (2026-09-23), fleet lead filing request. Verified on origin/master 2ed80789: every order-line writer stamps `unitsPerBox: boxes != null && upb > 1 ? upb : null` — create() :2129, :3485, :4539, :5350, :5424 (lineUpb), :5491 — so a NON-SPLIT (box-unaware) line on a boxed product is written with NO pack snapshot BY DESIGN. Every reader (invoicing, recompute, merge) then reads its qty as boxes of whatever the product's pack is NOW: a box-of-6 line bills as boxes of 12 after a 6→12 repack. This is SA-063's exposure, system-wide, and SA-104a closes it on the WRITE side: stamp unitsPerBox on EVERY line at write time, including non-split lines, so that a null snapshot means pre-era only. A design item — it touches every writer above and B297's PR plan (the writer-side counterpart; SA-104's 'never a third resolver' governs readers). Owner: Lane B, after B776. Money-adjacent — carve-out parked.
