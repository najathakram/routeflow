## Reported evidence

Follow-up from #3204 (S4/S1): settledAtShipment treats any stamped shippedAt as a real shipment, including one written by an earlier pre-ship tracking save. Rule: before switching an EXISTING tenant to ship/both/pickup_ship, run a read-only count of its orders with such a shippedAt; new onboarding tenants are unaffected.
