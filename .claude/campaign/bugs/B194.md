## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Typing 10 then scanning a box gives 10 units plus one box worth, per the normalize/rollover convention.

**Actually does.** The boxed increment branches rebuilt the line from the scan alone — the typed plain qty was discarded, so quantities came out “random” relative to what the operator had entered.

**The gap.** incrementLine/incrementLinePiece treated a plain qty and a boxes/pieces split as mutually exclusive instead of folding one into the other.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/mobile/lib/sale-line.ts; sale-line-fold.test.ts (REG-B194)
