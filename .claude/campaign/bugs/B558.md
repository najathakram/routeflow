## Reported evidence

Clicking 'Extend Trial' on a SUSPENDED tenant silently reactivates it to TRIAL status as a side effect -- not signposted anywhere on the button or in a confirmation step. The toast only says 'Trial extended -- new end: <date>', never mentions the status change. An operator suspending a tenant and later clicking Extend Trial (for an unrelated reason, or by habit) gets their suspension silently undone with no warning. Direct match for the owner's stated complaint: a consequential state change with no visible acknowledgment that it happened. Repro: suspend any test tenant, click Extend Trial, observe status flips SUSPENDED->TRIAL with only the trial-extension toast, nothing about reactivation.
