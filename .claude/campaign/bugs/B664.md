## Reported evidence

Chain: the product DTO validates unitsPerBox with @IsInt and no minimum, and inline edit saves any integer, so a product can carry unitsPerBox = 1. The web new-invoice form's addProductFromCatalog (~:863-865) reads 'product.unitsPerBox ? Number(product.unitsPerBox) : undefined' (1 is truthy), so it seeds boxes 1 / pieces 0, and :1081 and :1246 send boxes/pieces. On the server, manual-invoice-lines.ts (~52-66) guards on 'product?.unitsPerBox' (1 passes) and calls normalizeBoxesPieces WITHOUT qty; packages/pricing/src/pricing.ts:74 has 'hasBoxPackaging = upb > 1', so for upb 1 the helper returns qty 0. The line is stored with qty 0 and $0, and there is no zero-qty guard. Mobile and the web edit hydrate are guarded with '> 1'; the new-invoice form is not. It is the kind of defect discovered by an accountant, not a user. PRECONDITION: a product with unitsPerBox = 1. Whether any exists is a PROD data question: count active products with unitsPerBox = 1, then invoice items with qty = 0 and a productId on non-void invoices. FIX SHAPE (not a fix): one line in the form (treat upb <= 1 as no packaging) plus a spec that posts a unitsPerBox = 1 product through the form builder and asserts qty > 0; a server-side zero-qty guard is a separate call. HOW KNOWN: R-ui code-read; the Mac Lead confirmed the pricing rule (pricing.ts:74) and the form seed; the filer re-read the form line (:865), the server guard and the pricing rule on origin/master; NEVER RUN. Severity HIGH as ruled by the lead.
