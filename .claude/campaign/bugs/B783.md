## Root cause

Bug hunt 2026-11-06 (verify-api V3, read at 7be372afed), new detail, severity unchanged: completeStop (routes.service.ts:2117-2311) calls applyStopOutcomes (:2242) and never reconciles; the only call to reconcileDeliveredStopOrders is :2460 (completeWithPayment). For a REFUSED order the routed order's invoice is the pending-mirror DRAFT at the full ordered total, and once the order is REFUSED it is post-delivery, so resolvePendingMirror (apps/api/src/invoices/pending-mirror.ts:27-30) no longer treats it as a mirror: it becomes an unlocked, sendable DRAFT at the full total for goods the store refused. Credit exposure is NOT wrong: NON_BACKING_ORDER_STATUSES contains REFUSED (invoices/credit-exposure.ts:70-75), so that draft is excluded from exposure; the harm is a full-amount invoice someone can send. Reach: the route is live for OPERATOR and DRIVER (routes.controller.ts:189-199) but real callers today are QA and seed scripts (apps/api/scripts/qa-run.js:1596,1658, reports-qa.js:401, the fresh-data wipe script :846,934; scripts/data/no-caller-routes.json:488 lists it with no caller); mobile stopped calling the bare /complete in c5f579c2c6 (#273, 2026-07-16), so only a pre-July queued entry that never drained could reach it from a phone (the offline allow-list admits only complete-with-payment, apps/mobile/lib/offline-allowlist.ts:67). Interim until the SA-111 shape (delegate to completeWithPayment): call reconcileDeliveredStopOrders after applyStopOutcomes.
