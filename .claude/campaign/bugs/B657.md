## Reported evidence

Standing-order (template) and buyer bare-qty orders store a boxed product as boxes: null with qty counting BOXES (deliberate: orders.service.ts 2031-2039, order-templates.service.ts 442-455). The driver adjust screen (adjust.tsx ~554-559) seeds normalizeBoxesPieces({boxes: null, pieces: null, qty: 3, unitsPerBox: LIVE 12}), so 3 boxes are shown as '0 boxes + 3 pcs' and the door estimate is $10 instead of $120, shown to a driver standing in front of a customer. The steppers send an ABSOLUTE CHANGE_QTY qty = boxes x 12 + pieces (lib/at-door-diff.ts ~57-60) while the server keeps a boxes == null line in its own denomination (orders.service.ts 5327-5332: wasBoxSplit && upb > 1 ? normalizeBoxesPieces(...) : { qty: newQty, boxes: null }), so ONE 'Boxes +1' tap turns 3 boxes into 15 boxes, not 4. Nothing on the screen gates on li.boxes != null (the web order page does). MITIGATION: it goes through change-request approval, so an operator sees it before it applies. Precondition: a boxed product on a standing-order or buyer bare-qty order, with a driver at the door using Adjust. ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (shared with B641/B643/B644/B645/B646/B647/B655): the family is wider than callers omitting the split. COPIERS ALSO OMIT unitsPerBox (a snapshot), and anywhere a line is reconstructed from a bare {qty, unitPrice}/{productId, qty} without boxes/pieces/unitsPerBox, money or quantity is wrong by unitsPerBox; helpers such as computeLineSubtotal and normalizeBoxesPieces silently fall back to per-piece math. HOW KNOWN: code-read only, verified hop by hop by R-ui, key links re-read by the Mac Lead on origin/master 846bf492 (and the invoice duplicate(), mobile edit hydration, adjust.tsx seed, at-door-diff and orders.service.ts 5325-5334 hops re-read by the filer on origin/master); NEVER RUN. flag.units_v1 is OFF: live today.
