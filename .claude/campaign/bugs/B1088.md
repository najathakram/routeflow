## Reported evidence

On an annual subscription, enabling a new add-on is refused (409), but raising the quantity of an already-billed add-on goes through and adds a monthly Stripe item quantity. Amounts agree (Stripe prorates 50/unit/month; MRR +50/unit), so it does not bill wrongly; the guard and its comment are inconsistent. Ruling needed: refuse qty increases on annual, or allow monthly add-on items on annual subs and drop the 409. Found in the #3129 sandbox checklist.
