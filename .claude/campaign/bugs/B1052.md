## Reported evidence

From the lead (separate Opus refute-first pass; details in the lead's local hunt notes). applyCreditInTx writes an absolute amountUsed with no credit-note lock on the settle path; reachable through two office invoice paths, or office first then driver. Fix: lock the notes FOR UPDATE in id order in settleOrderCreditsInTx, write an increment, and do the same on restore. Test: a db.spec barrier case where the total stays at 100.
