# B421 · Credit note applied to an invoice is shown as 'Paid' everywhere, and counted as cash received in the bookkeeping cash-flow report

**Location** `apps/api/src/invoices/payment-predicates.ts:40 sumConfirmed() (choke point, no method filter); apps/api/src/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.service.ts:1018-1043 getCashFlow() totalIn` · **Severity** high · **Batch** F53 · **State** proven

## Summary

A CREDIT_NOTE-method InvoicePayment row (status: PAID) rendered and counted as cash everywhere a "Paid"/"collected"/"received" figure was computed -- invoice totals, the PDF, the buyer portal, both mobile screens, and every bookkeeping cash-flow/dashboard figure -- because the confirmed-payment predicate filtered by status only, never by method.

## What this feature is for

Invoice "Paid"/"received" figures and bookkeeping's cash-flow totals exist to tell operators how
much real cash a tenant has actually banked, separate from how much of an invoice's balance has
been settled by non-cash means -- a credit note or a drawn-down advance. Operators read them to
reconcile bank deposits and judge real liquidity (the numbers behind revenue-dashboard and
cash-flow reports); customers read the invoice/PDF/portal figures to know what they've paid versus
what they still owe. Cash and credit must never conflate because a CREDIT_NOTE application settles
an invoice's balance with no money changing hands -- counting it as cash overstates a tenant's real
collected revenue, can make a tenant believe they have more cash on hand than they do, and shows a
customer a false record of having paid money they never sent.

## Root cause

payment-predicates.ts's sumConfirmed() (the one choke point every consumer called) answered "is this payment confirmed" (status===PAID), which is necessary but not sufficient for "is this cash the tenant received" -- a CREDIT_NOTE application is a real, confirmed payment row that correctly reduces the invoice's balance, but it represents a credit extended, not tender collected. Nothing in the codebase distinguished the two questions, so every consumer of sumConfirmed silently answered both with the same number.

## User impact

Client-reported case: an invoice with a $638 return credit note applied showed "Paid to date -$638.00" and "Paid $638.00" although the customer never paid anything. The same conflation reached the customer-facing PDF, the buyer's own portal view (which had a second, separate bug: it also counted unconfirmed DRAFT rows), both mobile invoice screens, and -- the most severe instance -- every tenant-wide cash-flow and revenue-dashboard figure in bookkeeping.service.ts (getCashFlow.totalIn, weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly receipts, totalCollected), meaning a tenant's own bookkeeping reports overstated real cash received by the amount of every credit note applied tenant-wide.

## Fix approach and UX

Added splitConfirmed(payments) -> {cash, creditApplied, advanceApplied} and resolveConfirmedAmounts(precomputed, payments) (all-or-nothing on a precomputed totalPaid, never mixing bases -- see lesson L-159) to a new @routeflow/pricing/src/payment-confirmation.ts (money discipline: one shared copy, api/web/mobile all import it, no per-app mirrors), plus RECEIVED_METHOD_FILTER (excludes only CREDIT_NOTE; ADVANCE stays in a cash-received figure since its application is the only place already-real cash is ever recorded) and CASH_METHOD_FILTER (excludes both, for an invoice-level cash-only figure). Wording: operator surfaces show "Payments received" (cash-only) alongside separate, neutral-styled "Credits applied"/"Advance applied" lines; customer-facing surfaces (PDF, buyer portal, mobile) show "Credit issued"/"Advance applied". Balance/status/outstanding figures were left on the FULL confirmed total throughout (a credit note or advance genuinely settles what's owed) -- fixed only the DISPLAY-as-cash sites. 9 surfaces fixed across api/web/mobile; per-surface detail in the code-map (feature-modules-1/3/6/7.md, web/routes-1/3.md, mobile/screens-by-role.md). Two related findings ruled OUT of B421's scope and filed/handled separately: an invoice settled by a RETURN credit note still counting its full total as accrual revenue (a real but distinct revenue-recognition question), and bookkeeping/invoice-template.tsx's dead Transaction-model pathway (masks an unrelated broken PDF download for real tenants).

## Test plan

Red-first REG-B421 cases per surface: packages/pricing/src/payment-confirmation.spec.ts (the core split + all-or-nothing fallback math); apps/api/src/invoices/{payment-predicates,invoice-pdf.service,invoices.service}.spec.ts; apps/api/src/customers/customers.service.spec.ts (lifetimeReceived + getIncomeChart method-filter assertions); apps/api/src/buyer/statement.service.spec.ts (CREDIT_NOTE + ADVANCE both route to the credits bucket); apps/api/src/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.service.spec.ts (getCashFlow + a cross-cutting case proving getMobileDashboard/getSummary/getFinanceDashboard agree); apps/web (InvoiceTotalsSummary.test.tsx RTL wording assertions, resolve-invoice-list-balance.test.ts, buyer portal page.b421-balance.test.tsx); apps/mobile/**tests**/invoice-credit-advance-pins.test.ts (source-text pins, no DOM under mobile Jest). 618+ tests passing across every touched file; full api typecheck clean at every commit.

## Reported evidence

Client-reported (tenant identity removed): an invoice with a return credit note applied shows 'Paid to date -$638.00' and 'Paid $638.00' although the customer never paid anything -- the $638 is a credit note applied to the invoice. Root cause (verified against master fbec7b7b): credit-notes.service.ts creates a real InvoicePayment row (method: CREDIT_NOTE, status: PAID); payment-predicates.ts:40 sumConfirmed() sums every status===PAID row with NO method filter, and invoices.service.ts (list :3073, detail :3304) derive paidAmount/balanceDue from it. Only per-row labels (Payment History card, PDF row, bookkeeping void/CN reconciliation) read method -- no TOTAL does. Conflates across web invoice detail/list, invoice PDF, buyer portal (also double-counts DRAFT via a separate status!==VOID reduce), mobile customer/operator invoice, customers detail, buyer statement, and bookkeeping summary. MONEY REPORT DEFECT: bookkeeping cash-flow's totalIn (verified :1018-1043, payments fetched with status:PAID and no method filter, summed directly) counts a credit-note application as cash received. Fix design (Fable, brief local-assets/handoff/2026-09-14/LAUNCH-credit-applied-vs-paid.md): add splitConfirmed() returning {cash, creditApplied, advanceApplied} next to sumConfirmed; paidAmount becomes cash-only, new creditApplied/advanceApplied fields on the invoice DTO in packages/types/api; wording splits into 'Payments received' + 'Credits applied' (operator) / 'Credit issued -- CN-...' (customer-facing); cash-flow totalIn becomes cash-methods-only; buyer portal's DRAFT-counting reduce replaced with the confirmed-only predicate. Full surface list, rulings and red-first REG-B421 test plan in the brief.
