Observed on the Addons & Features tab. Owner: 'It moves around and also not proper at all.' Screenshot: C:\Users\nakram\.claude\uploads\20f39e54-c9b5-4469-a28a-cb7ec44dff13\01a61a6c-image.jpg. Owner expects other platform-admin pages share it -- a full sweep of the surface is running separately. (2) The QA-sweep blind spot: this exact surface's own QA sweep reported 33/33 clean at 390/768/1440 earlier today, including 'no document-level horizontal overflow' -- but the owner is looking at exactly that defect on exactly that surface right now. Either the sweep visited the tenant LIST page and not the tenant DETAIL page, or documentElement.scrollWidth > clientWidth does not detect this class of overflow (page-level shift/scroll rather than a single element overflowing). This is the FOURTH instance today of a check reporting clean while not actually checking the thing it claims to -- now the fleet's dominant defect class (see B540 for the sibling overflow-hidden-table class, and the L-202/L-205 lessons for two other same-day instances of this exact pattern).
