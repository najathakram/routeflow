## Reported evidence

On a two-line $200 invoice (line A $100), a credit note of $33.33 (3 units) followed by a return of all 10 units refunded at $100 credits line A $133.33; both writes are accepted (201). Single-line invoices are blocked only by the invoice-level headroom check. Cause: ReturnsService.processRefund mints a lump-sum credit note with no items (amount from billedBasisFor) and checks only the invoice headroom; CreditNotesService.create's per-line cap counts only item-bearing credit notes, and the returns cap does not subtract prior credit-note credits. Found by the F2 proof on master d486363d (#2554 comment 5926574557). Money, tier-1.
