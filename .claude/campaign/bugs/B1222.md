## Reported evidence

A super-admin can switch a tenant into or out of a ship-billing fulfilment mode (ship / both / pickup_ship) while SHIP orders are shipped but not delivered; those orders then bill at the wrong moment (at shipment vs at delivery) because billing reads the CURRENT mode. The tenant self-serve toggle (fulfilment PR1) refuses this with FULFILMENT_CARRIER_IN_TRANSIT; the console/profile paths do not. Real fix = a billedAtShipment column captured per order (fulfilment PR2, needs a migration sitting). SA-725 M1.
