## Reported evidence

The probe measures in-process heapUsed plus arrayBuffers growth against a 50 MB bound with no warm-up, so the result depends on test order and heap state. It passes inside the whole-file run, but run alone (jest -t "200 concurrent") it fails 6 of 6 with 78-89 MB growth, and it failed once under load in the pre-push verify (turbo, @routeflow/control#test) before passing on the rerun. Once #2900 merges it can false-red every PR Verify. Fix plan: a tests-only commit on stacked PR #2905 that makes the probe order-independent (warm-up flood, then measure) and still goes red on a deliberately buffered 200 MB body. Filed via the control-system lane.
