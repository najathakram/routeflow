## Reported evidence

NUKE_MODELS has no entry for CommissionAccrual, CommissionPayout, CustomerCommissionRate, AgentAssignment, SupplierProduct or SupplierCredit, so deleting the parent rows of a tenant that has any of them fails with a foreign-key violation (P2003). It also orders returnRecord after order and payment after transaction, so those deletes hit their referencing rows first. Separately, reset-and-seed.ts names Prisma delegates that no longer exist. Fix: add the six models before their parents, reorder the two, drop the dead delegates, and add a spec that walks the schema so a new tenant-scoped model cannot be missed. Test tenants only.
