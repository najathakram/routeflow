## Root cause

Reproduced on master ebc237be0 (test tenant, seeded buyer, order opened in edit mode). At 390 px the header row is PRODUCT x=25, QTY x=132, SUBTOTAL x=272 (the Unit Price th is max-sm:hidden), but the edit row renders Unit Price ($14.99) at x=272 and the real subtotal ($29.98) at x=367, off the 390 px viewport; at 1440 the columns line up ($14.99 / $29.98). Cause: the edit-row Unit Price td at page.tsx:993 lacks max-sm:hidden, which the read-only row (line 1067) has. Display-only: the saved subtotal is not affected. Fix: add max-sm:hidden to that td.
