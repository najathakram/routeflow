## Reported evidence

cancel()/resume() only wrote TenantSubscription.cancelAtPeriodEnd locally — Stripe was never told, so a tenant who cancelled in-app kept being billed by Stripe with no way to notice. Zero prod exposure at fix time (no tenant yet holds stripeSubId). Fixed: cancel()/resume() now propagate cancel_at_period_end to Stripe BEFORE the local transaction commits.
