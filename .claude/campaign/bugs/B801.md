## Root cause

Fleet lead relay, 2026-09-23. Verified on origin/master b8863d56. In extractExpenseItems, each OCR item is written with amount: Number(item.amount ?? item.unitCost ?? 0) (:896), totalFromItems = items.reduce((s, i) => s + Number(i.amount ?? 0), 0) (:903), and the expense is then updated with amount: totalFromItems (:909). None of these goes through roundMoney, so a float sum (0.1 + 0.2) lands as the expense amount. The same class as B781 (#1301, the createExpense mileage and itemized path). Money rule: round every monetary write. Fix: roundMoney per line and on the total, with a red-first spec. Owner: the unit-picker lane can take it after B780 and the #1300/#1301 rounds.
