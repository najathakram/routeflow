## Reported evidence

SA-1156 section 0 item 3 (observe first, own PR, not #4321): mergeEligibleWhere('force') treats only a non-DRAFT invoice as billed, so a loser whose open DRAFT mirror holds a live payment (cash or a credit) is eligible and its DRAFT is removed with it (the B214 exception). That may strand or drop the recorded payment. Probe on the test tenant: a loser with a DRAFT mirror and CASH 5, plus a second loser with a DRAFT and a credit; force consolidate; report each payment row, the note's amountUsed and the invoice rows. Fix shape if confirmed: the force predicate reads invoices none EDIT_BLOCKING_INVOICE_WHERE beside BILLED_INVOICE (the same fragment); a credit-only DRAFT stays eligible only if the probe shows B214's removal releases the credit.
