# B322 · Bulk mark-paid reports a bill as skipped after its payment committed, and can leave an expense PAID while reporting failure

## Summary

Bulk mark-paid reports a bill as skipped after its payment committed, and can leave an expense PAID while reporting failure

## What this feature is for

Context: Bookkeeping · API POST /bookkeeping/bills/bulk-mark-paid (web vendor-bills bulk action). This is part of the F41 batch ("Bulk mark-paid reports a bill as skipped after its payment c…") — see the batch theme in the hunt digest for the surrounding feature's purpose.

## Root cause

Bulk mark-paid reports a bill as skipped after its payment committed, and can leave an expense PAID while reporting failure

Symptom / evidence: A throw after payBillFully commits pushes the id into skipped, so the run's reported cash total understates what left the bank; on the unlinked branch the expense is already PAID yet reported skipped, and paid++ never adds to totalAmount.

Files implicated: apps/api/src/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.service.ts, apps/web/app/(dashboard)/vendor-bills/page.tsx.
