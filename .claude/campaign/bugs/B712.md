## Reported evidence

applyScheduledCancellations (:403-455) snapshots then does bare writes; resume() (:1073-1082) is also a bare update. Interleave leaves Stripe resumed while the tenant is locked READ_ONLY, and resume() then 409s on retry. Cite B561 as the sibling class. Fix: count-gated tenantSubscription.updateMany({tenantId, cancelAtPeriodEnd:true, periodEnd<now}) inside the same tx as the tenant write, before it. Found by MacBook's A5 concurrency verifier.
