## Root cause

SA-130 residual (2026-09-23), from the #1319 (B810) review. On the RECONCILED path, a DRAFT invoice that already carries a confirmed payment (e.g. an applied advance) is re-derived at its delivered-basis total WITHOUT subtracting that payment, while the server applies only the remaining balance. The driver is told to collect the draft's paid part again, and the surplus lands as an advance (money stays correct; the display over-asks). Shape (SA): subtract (draft.total − draft.balanceDue) from the reconciled estimate for that draft, using the DRAFT row the payable list (B810) already carries. Lands after #1319.
