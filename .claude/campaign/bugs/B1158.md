## Reported evidence

Must fix before Stripe goes live. A superseded stored subscription in status incomplete may hold a bank debit that is still processing; cancelling it can let that payment settle while the replacement also charges, so the tenant pays twice. Fix: read latest_invoice.payment_intent.status; if processing do not cancel and raise a notice (the owner keeps bank debits); re-read the status right before the cancel. Found in review of #3226.
