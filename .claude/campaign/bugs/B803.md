# B803 · Re-delivery plan = remaining qty: neither the at-stop close path nor short-pick can record a REMAINDER delivery, so a second visit can't plan and deliver only the undelivered qty of a partially delivered order

## Root cause

SA-115 filing (fleet lead relay, 2026-09-23). Verified on origin/master b8863d56. RUN_LINE_ITEMS_SELECT (routes.constants.ts) carries qty and status but no deliveredQty, so the stop's embedded snapshot can't see what an earlier visit delivered. deliveryTypeForQty(deliveredQty, orderedQty) (short-pick.ts:63-65) classifies against the full ORDERED qty. The rich short-pick path EXCLUDES already-delivered lines (deliveredQty != 0) rather than planning their remainder. So on a re-delivery stop, an order partially delivered earlier (say 6 of 10) can't be planned as 'deliver the remaining 4'. The rich path drops the line, and B762's snapshot fallback for an un-landed order re-claims full delivery (an accepted residual in SA-115). Needs: deliveredQty on the stop snapshot select, a plan qty of qty - deliveredQty, and a server contract for a remainder delivery entry consistent with SA-109/109a and SA-113b's evidence rows. Pairs with B762 and B785. No owner yet.

The remaining qty is served by the API, order status is out of scope, and there's no reopen. Build on B762. Sequence: #1294 → B762 → #1274 round 7 and B785 → B803.
