## Reported evidence

Since B100/F16b, reserveNext for INVOICE/ESTIMATE/CREDIT_NOTE uses a per-tenant-year series that lazy-seeds from built-in DEFAULTS (INV-/EST-/CN-, pad 4) and the tenant's own <PREFIX>-<year>-#### documents; the year-0 row the Numbering card writes (prefix, next number, padding) is never read on that path, and seedFromSource has no callers. A migrating wholesaler who sets 'next invoice 10543' still gets INV-2026-0001, and the card's preview shows a number live minting will never produce. Either the card drives the per-year series or its copy stops promising continuity.
