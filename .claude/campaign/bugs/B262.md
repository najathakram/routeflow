## Reported evidence

Across the 15 reskinned auth pages, the operator and buyer surfaces disagree on copy that reads as inconsistent rather than intentional: the divider between social and primary sign-in reads OR on some pages and or on others, the back-to-website link carries an arrow glyph on the buyer pages but not on the operator pages' equivalent link, and /reset-password has no footer link where its sibling auth pages do. All three are fenced, page-owned, verbatim strings per the auth-redesign spec, so none were changed.
