## Reported evidence

A failed offline save of a setting or money action shows "failed" to the user but replays on reconnect. Seen three times: credit notes (#2867), settings (#2868), REG-B308. Fix: flip the default so only an explicit allow-list of offline-safe endpoints (driver POD and stop updates etc.) may queue and everything else is online-only, plus a static test that any newly queued endpoint must be on the list. Systemic. Owner: N-kit after its web bug PR.
