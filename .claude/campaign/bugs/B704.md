## Reported evidence

returns.service.ts:619-712 priceReturn uses invItem.subtotal only. processRefund (:802-807) mints a lump-sum credit note at that subtotal-only amount. The inline return path correctly refunds subtotal+tax+categoryTax. Example: a line of 50 subtotal + 10 tax + 4 category tax -> standard-return CN is 50.00, customer is short 14.00. Fix shape pending SA (a CN-cap change). High, fast-follow.
