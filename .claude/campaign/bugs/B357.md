# B357 · X-Forwarded-For spoofing bypasses per-IP throttling and forges audit IP (F9-006)

**Location** `API · rate-limit (trust proxy / throttler tracker)` · **Severity** medium · **Batch** F48 · **State** queued

## Summary

_Not yet analysed._

## What this feature is for

_Not yet analysed._

## Root cause

trust proxy=2 plus the default tracker reads the leftmost forwarded entry; attacker-appended entries shift the trusted hop.

## User impact

Login throttle bypass (brute force) and forged IPs in the audit log.

## Fix approach and UX

Custom getTracker and audit-IP resolver that take the hop at the known depth from the right (or Railway's verified client-IP header); normalize or reject malformed chains.

## Test plan

T1: a crafted 3+ entry chain is bucketed by the real hop and audited as such; login throttle still trips at the configured limit.

## Reported evidence

trust proxy=2 with the default tracker takes the leftmost forwarded address, so a client-appended header moves the trusted hop. Expected: the hop at the known depth from Railway's edge (or its verified client-IP header) is used for both throttling and audit.
