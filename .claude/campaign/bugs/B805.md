## Root cause

Fleet lead ruling (2026-09-23), the #1294 (B775) follow-up that B707 deferred and flagged in 279b9e07, plus Opus's note (c) on #1294. After #1294 (merged 8d94dd69), only addPickedToDraft advances draftRef ref-first (SA-108d); the other draft writers (setQty, remove, the price modal, the substitute) call setDraft directly, so draftRef lags until the next render. That isn't a live bug today, because draftRef is read only by addPickedToDraft. But remove X followed by scan X in one batch can resolve the scan against a stale mirror, and Save must handle a key that is both present in the draft and queued in pendingDeletes. Fix: route every draft writer through one updateDraft(fn) that advances draftRef ref-first (the SA-108d shape), and make Save's diff treat present-and-pending-delete deterministically (the re-added row wins and the delete is cancelled). Red-first pure specs plus source pins. Owner: B707, after B762.
