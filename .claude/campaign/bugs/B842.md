## Root cause

Fleet lead relay 2026-09-24, from SA-135b (#1330 / B646 PASS at 09fa9253). Before #1330, every cycle of a recurring template line that was box-intent AND category-taxed stored the box count in the pieces column, so its regulated-ledger SALE rows carried qty-in-boxes as units (e.g. 6 where 72 pieces were sold): a 12x UNDER-report of units, and of the per-piece levy, in periods already filed. #1330 fixes it going forward (the subtotal is unchanged; the per-piece levy on those lines rises 12x to the correct figure). This record covers the PAST-period remedy only.

CORRECTION, SA-135c (2026-09-24, SA error E55): invoice MONEY was never wrong. manual-invoice-lines.ts :141-142 already levied a no-split boxed line on qty x pack, so the levy and every line's money are unchanged by #1330 (pinned by the lane's equality spec). B842 is a regulated-ledger UNITS under-report (and the COGS basis), NOT a levy under-report. #1330's PR wording (the lead's): 'ledger units and COGS basis change; invoice money unchanged (pinned)'. The past-period remedy question (an amended units figure in filed periods) stays with the owner and compliance.
