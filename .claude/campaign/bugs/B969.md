# B969 · A return on a legacy box-unaware line (order line counts boxes, invoice item counts pieces) under-refunds by the pack size: itemiseRefund and priceReturn price off billedSubtotal/billedQty

**Location** `apps/api/src/returns/returns.service.ts (itemiseRefund ~:826 and priceReturn ~:940: billedSubtotal/billedQty on a box-unaware line); credit note refund` · **Severity** high · **Batch** F49 · **State** already-fixed

## Summary

SEVERITY CONFIRMED HIGH (money) by SA-628p (#2554 5939135736), via Mac Lead: a STANDARD return on a box-UNAWARE selling-unit boxed line is priced off the invoice's billedSubtotal/billedQty (per piece), so 1 box refunds $5.00 instead of $60.00, an UNDER-refund. The fix prices in pieces on both sides (MacBook, its own PR after #2988). The owner gets a count-only sizing of past under-refunds; remediation is the owner's call. Board issue: #2992.

## Reported evidence

From the lead's Opus review of #2988 (#2554 verdict-2988) plus F2's fixture read (SA-628o's 'separate HIGH'), via the lead. Severity HIGH pending the SA's ruling. Pre-existing on master. On legacy orders where the order line counts BOXES but its invoice item counts PIECES (box-unaware), itemiseRefund and priceReturn price off billedSubtotal/billedQty, so returning 1 box of 12 refunds $5 instead of $60: an under-refund by the pack size (an under-credit to the customer).
