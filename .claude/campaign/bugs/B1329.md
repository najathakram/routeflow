## Reported evidence

A one-off RF_HEAVY_SLOT_MAX_HOLD_MIN raise on the verify run makes the heavy-slot self-test fail, because I5d (status flags a holder over the cap) and I5e (the hold cap is honoured) read the cap from the inherited environment. The test should clear that variable before it spawns the slot, so a caller's raise cannot change what it measures.
