## Root cause

Proven live 2026-09-23 on the Mac mini during a disk-full recovery: janitor report listed rf-lane-R-ui-b735 (B735, active, 5 uncommitted files) and rf-lane-vendor-bill-pack (B707's B743, active, 9 uncommitted files) as '(worktree …, merged)' and planned to clear their node_modules — both are freshly cut lanes with 0 commits ahead. The inverse also fails: squash-merged branches (#1250, #1252, #1262) were reported 'not merged' because their commits are not ancestors of master. Merged must be decided by the PR state from gh (and local HEAD == the merged PR's headRefOid), never by ancestry or by '0 ahead'. Also: clearing node_modules under a worktree with uncommitted work should be refused outright.
