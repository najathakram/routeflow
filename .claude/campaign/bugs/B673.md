## Reported evidence

The ADD_ITEM change-request payload carries boxes/pieces, but approveNextDelivery ignores them and creates the next-delivery line from a bare qty. A boxed ADD_ITEM therefore lands as a selling-unit count instead of the boxed split — the same class as B655. Found by S-scan while fixing B655 (#1174). Owner: S-scan.
