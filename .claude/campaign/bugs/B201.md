## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Once the operator clears the field (or the code was submitted), a late-settling lookup can't act.

**Actually does.** The settled exact-match auto-add (for no-terminator wedge scanners) re-fired when a slow refetch settled after the field had already been cleared — adding an item nobody just scanned.

**The gap.** The auto-add was gated on a time window instead of on the identity of the scan attempt it belonged to.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/mobile/components/NewOrderScreen.tsx (autoAddAttemptRef) + lib/wedge-submit.ts createScanAttempt; REG-B201
