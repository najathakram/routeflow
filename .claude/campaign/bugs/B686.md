## Reported evidence

apps/api/tsconfig.json's exclude array contains prisma/** (alongside spec/test files, prisma.config.ts and scripts/**), so apps/api/prisma/seed.ts has never been type-checked or linted by the normal workspace commands -- not before this change and not after. Discovered by U-units while building #1223 (the units-flag seed grant): they had to type-check the file with a throwaway tsconfig override to verify their own change, and documented the gap in the commit/PR body rather than letting the next person rediscover it. The consequence is not cosmetic: seed.ts contains assertSafeTarget() and assertTestTenant() -- the guards that stop a seed script being pointed at a production database. A mistake in that file, including in the guards themselves, is caught by nothing automated (no tsc, no eslint). The exclusion may well be deliberate, since prisma/** also holds the generated Prisma client, so the likely fix direction is to type-check the hand-written scripts under prisma/ (seed.ts and any siblings) without pulling in generated output, rather than removing the exclusion wholesale. Fifth instance of the same pattern found in one release-night sweep alongside B682/B683/B684/B685: a control that exists, is believed to be working, and does not run on the thing it is supposed to protect. Fast-follow, not release-blocking. Filed per the fleet lead's request.
