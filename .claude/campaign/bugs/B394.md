## Reported evidence

POST /orders with discountAmount greater than subtotal (e.g. 1000 on a $20 subtotal) persists an order with a negative total (e.g. -980); GET /orders/{id} confirms it's durable, not just echoed. InvoicesService.create rejects the equivalent invoice-level case with HTTP 400, but OrdersService.create has no such check.
