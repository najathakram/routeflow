## Reported evidence

Confirmed by Mac Lead: saving a PO edit that touches only notes (no line changes) silently rewrites a bill-raised line's exact stored total (11,000.00 -> 11,000.40). Same root mechanism as B690 (now refuted as a repro artifact, but its code-read stands and is moved here): buildUpdateItems in purchase-orders-edit.ts sends the LIVE product pack as packSize and derives the split from it, ignoring the line's own stored packSize -- so ANY save, even a notes-only one, re-derives and can silently change a line's money even when the operator touched nothing about that line. SA-059's ruling attaches: an edit must derive from the line's OWN stored packSize; the live product pack is for NEW lines only. Silent stored-money change, not merely a display bug -- cross-link B690 for the DRAFT-double-edit symptom that shares this cause (that symptom itself did not reproduce on a fresh master API image and is refuted separately, but the underlying mechanism is real and lives here).
