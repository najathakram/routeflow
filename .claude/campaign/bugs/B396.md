## Reported evidence

Self-service signup for a trial tenant creates no TenantSubscription row; cancel() previously required that row to exist, so a trial or read-only tenant with no row could never cancel in-app. Fixed: cancel() now runs a compare-and-swap directly on Tenant.status (works with or without a row); READ_ONLY calls are an idempotent no-op; a lost CAS re-read as READ_ONLY still succeeds idempotently, re-read as ACTIVE refuses instead of overwriting a paying tenant.
