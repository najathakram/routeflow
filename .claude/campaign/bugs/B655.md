## Reported evidence

For CHANGE_QTY, approveNextDelivery computes delta = Number(payload.newQty) - Number(li.qty) (~312), both in PIECES for a box-split line (the sibling APPLY_NOW branch feeds newQty to normalizeBoxesPieces({ qty: newQty, unitsPerBox }), orders.service.ts ~5333). It then calls ordersService.create({ items: [{ productId, qty: delta }], status: "DRAFT" }, buyerPseudoUser) (~355-357) with a BARE qty and the pseudo-user typed BUYER, where a bare qty counts SELLING UNITS (REG-B47; the buyer.controller.ts comment says 'never loose pieces — create() stores such a line box-unaware and bills qty x the BOX price'). Example: 6 boxes of 12 (qty 72) changed to 96 pieces gives delta 24, which becomes 24 BOXES at $40 = $960 instead of 2 boxes = $80. The stock decrement runs on the same wrong quantity when the draft is promoted (orders.service.ts ~2695). ADD_ITEM drops payload.boxes/pieces the same way (the spec pins items: [{productId, qty: 3}]). Precondition: an at-door or customer CHANGE_QTY increase on a boxed line resolved NEXT_DELIVERY. The result is a DRAFT, and the unit fence covers Case (unit-aware) lines only. THE CODE COMMENT THAT NAMES THE ROOT CAUSE, verbatim, change-requests.service.ts lines ~309-311: // units_v1: the delta is in the line's own unit axis but is re-created through create() as // pack selling units at the live price — wrong for a unit-aware line. unitAware.assertNoUnitAwareLines([li], "rolled to the next delivery"); The hazard was documented accurately and fenced for the unit-aware case only; the identical problem for an ordinary box-split line sat in the same three lines, unguarded. HOW KNOWN: code-read only, verified by R-ui, the Mac Lead and the fleet lead on origin/master; NEVER RUN. flag.units_v1 is NOT required: this is live today for ordinary boxed lines. ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (shared with B641/B644/B645/B646/B647): anywhere a line is reconstructed from a bare {qty, unitPrice}/{productId, qty} without carrying boxes/pieces (and unitsPerBox), money or quantity is wrong by unitsPerBox; this is the sixth independent reconstruction site (Duplicate B644, estimate convert B645, recurring B646, standing orders B647, and this one). CANDIDATES, NOT VERIFIED BY ANYONE: (a) invoice duplicate() also drops the unitsPerBox snapshot (the subtotal stays right; the copy then depends on the live pack); (b) replenishment / shelf.lowItems under-orders (a sweeper says it reads box-unaware history as pieces); (c) staff merge reads a stored box-unaware line as loose pieces (merge-items.ts documents 'gap 2, filed and known').
