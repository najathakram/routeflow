## Reported evidence

N2's spec (NOTIFY-SPEC.md) calls for a notice to the OLD address plus verification of the NEW address before it becomes the login. updateUser currently just writes dto.email straight into the row with zero verification and zero notification -- an admin (or a compromised admin session) can silently repoint any user's login email with no signal reaching the account's real owner at their original address.
