## Reported evidence

Board #2070 (found by the Opus pass on #2061). The remaining quantity is read before the transaction and the invoicedQty increment is unconditional, so two operators billing the last 96 pieces at once both pass and invoicedQty ends at 192 on a 96-piece line. Fix: make the check and increment atomic (conditional update in the transaction or a row lock on the order line; never a second in-process lock), with a DB-lane concurrency spec that fails on the old code.
