## Root cause

Found in the read-only StockLot audit (S-scan, 2026-09-20, master 8f44bf3b). Six stockLot.create sites: inventory recordPurchase (reference and notes from the DTO), recordAdjustment (reference ?? 'ADJUSTMENT', notes from the DTO), variant assign (reference VARIANT_ASSIGN-<uuid>, server), stock count (STOCK_COUNT-<sessionId>), PO receive (po.poNumber, server-generated), and vendor-bills receive (bill.billNumber, user-typed, unique per tenant). Readers keyed on reference: reverseBillLots (vendor-bills.service.ts:1269, called at :1239 unreceive and :1358 void), stockMovement.deleteMany at :1207, returns.service.ts:893 (RET-<id8>). Headline repro (UNPROVEN by a DB test): a bill numbered ADJUSTMENT, once unreceived or voided, deletes or zeroes every default-reference adjustment lot in the tenant. The dangerous path is accidental, not adversarial. It changes FIFO/LIFO cost layers and the ledger; it does not create stock or money. Nothing parses LOT notes; the only notes parse is reversePoReceipts (B608, being deleted) on MOVEMENT notes; vendor-bills.service.ts:1280 only appends to lot.notes (latent trap, not a vector). Fix direction (endorsed by the fleet lead, schema change, after the deadline): stop keying reversal on free-text reference; use sourceType + sourceId columns, or a server-only reference namespace DTO input cannot write.

## Reported evidence

reverseBillLots finds lots with stockLot.findMany({ where: { reference: billNumber } }) — no productId and no source scoping — then deletes them or sets remainingQty 0. reference is free text also written by recordPurchase and recordAdjustment (default 'ADJUSTMENT'), and billNumber is user-typed, so unreceiving or voiding a bill whose number equals another flow's reference removes those lots tenant-wide (every default adjustment lot for 'ADJUSTMENT', a PO's receive lots for a PO number). Same shape: stockMovement.deleteMany on (productId, reference: billNumber, PURCHASE) at :1207 and on RET-<id8> at returns.service.ts:893. Changes FIFO/LIFO cost layers and the ledger; creates no stock or money. Unproven by a DB test.
