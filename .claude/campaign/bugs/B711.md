## Reported evidence

billing-cron.service.ts:74-92 does a bare tenant.update by id with an awaited email per tenant. subscribe() (:346-356) never clears trialEndsAt. A tenant that subscribes DURING the sweep is locked READ_ONLY by the cron's stale write, and a second subscribe double-counts ledgerMrr (mrr.service.ts:22). Cite B561 as the sibling class. Fix: tenant.updateMany gated on status===TRIAL AND trialEndsAt<now; skip the event/email when the update matched 0 rows. Found by MacBook's A5 concurrency verifier. Fast-follow.
