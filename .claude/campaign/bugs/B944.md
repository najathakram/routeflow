## Reported evidence

When the primary already has an appleId, the secondary Apple refresh token is discarded without calling Apple /auth/revoke, leaving a live Apple grant for an account that no longer exists. Fix: revoke the secondary token at Apple during the merge (best effort, logged). Not folded into #2910; filed via Mac Lead.
