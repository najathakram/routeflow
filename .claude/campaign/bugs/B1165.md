## Reported evidence

After a Stripe subscription is cancelled the add-on rows keep their stripeItemId pointer. A later re-subscribe then reads the add-on as already billed (item present) while the new subscription has no such item, so the add-on runs free. Distinct from B107 (disableAddon nulling the pointer on a failed delete).
