## Root cause

Found by the apple-ux audit of 2026-11-24 (web at master 93bc7c4025, local stack, tenant test). Steps: a product with unit can and 12 pieces per box, price 1.25: the page reads PRICE PER CAN (RETAIL) 1.25 with a line under it reading about 0.10 / pc, and every tier the same. A product with unit piece and 24 per box reads Price per piece (retail) 1.50 with about 0.06 / pc. An order of 24 took 288 pieces of stock, so the stored price is per box while the label names the piece unit. A primary-school reader enters the piece price in a box-price field. Units, HIGH: goes to the SA, not into a UI batch. Related known item Q12 (Price per can on a box line).
