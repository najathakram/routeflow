## Reported evidence

A concurrent Pricing change to a subscription's negotiated fee can race a plan upgrade: the upgrade's optimistic guard matches only on tenantId+planKey, not priceOverrideMonthly, so it can silently apply the upgrade's basePriceSnapshot over a fee that changed underneath it. (GitHub #2470)
