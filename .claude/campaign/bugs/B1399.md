## Root cause

b58-guard.ts:38-41 does not refuse a cycle change on a row that has a provider subscription; subscribe() writes cycle and period itself.

_Not yet analysed._

## Fix approach and UX

BP-1b (SA-1304 R-1/W-0 ruling, queue line 2): subscribeCrossCycleRefusal refuses a cycle change on a provider-billed row with CYCLE_SWITCH_AT_RENEWAL_ONLY (409) until PR-6.

## Test plan

T1. REG-B1399-cross-cycle-subscribe: a provider-billed yearly row picking MONTHLY is refused, 0 writes; a manual yearly row is still allowed.

## Reported evidence

The B58 guard lets a cross-cycle pick through. Master writes cycle ANNUAL and a one-year periodEnd on a provider-billed monthly row with 0 provider calls. After B1406's refusal of every ANNUAL entry the remaining case is the reverse: a provider-billed yearly tenant picking MONTHLY.
