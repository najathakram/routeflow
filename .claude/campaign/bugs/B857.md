## Reported evidence

The tenant detail page hardcodes PLANS = [STARTER, PROFESSIONAL, ENTERPRISE]. planKeyFromEnum maps PROFESSIONAL to SCALE, so GROWTH (a public tier) and LITE are unselectable in both the plan control and the Manual Activation form, although the API accepts them (SELECTABLE_TENANT_PLANS). A bank/Zelle/check payer on GROWTH can only be activated on the wrong plan (wrong entitlements and MRR). The create form is catalog-driven and unaffected.
