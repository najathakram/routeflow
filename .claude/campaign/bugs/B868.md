## Fix approach and UX

OWNER RULING 2026-09-25: resume restores the downgrade the tenant had scheduled (their earlier choice is honoured, consistent with the B216 rule that nobody quietly undoes a tenant-chosen downgrade). Fix shape: cancel() must not discard downgradeToPlanKey/downgradeEffectiveAt (or must stash them), and resume() must re-arm them when the effective date is still in the future; if it has passed, apply at the next cycle boundary. Repro: local end-to-end billing QA step 9 (2026-09-25).

## Reported evidence

Local end-to-end run (2026-09-25 billing QA, step 9): after scheduling GROWTH→STARTER, cancel() itself nulls downgradeToPlanKey/downgradeEffectiveAt; resume() then leaves the tenant on GROWTH with no downgrade, so the next cycle bills 249 instead of the 99 the tenant chose. The B216 ruling protects a tenant-chosen downgrade from admin/webhook paths; whether the tenant's own cancel should also preserve it (and resume restore it) is an owner call.
