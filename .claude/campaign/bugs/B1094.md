## Reported evidence

No Idempotency-Key on POST /buyer/orders and the merge lock only serializes, so a retried submit merges again and doubles quantities; both clients keep the cart and say try again; the post-commit sweep rethrows. Fix shape: per-cart-session key checked inside the lock (merge-idempotency.ts helpers); post-commit errors logged, never rethrown. Verified by an independent refute-first pass; live for every tenant.
