## Root cause

From the #1318 (B800) review, fleet lead relay 2026-09-23. Verified on origin/master 361e46eb (2026-09-23). safeRemoveTree (:179-184) runs 'cmd /c rmdir <junction>' then 'cmd /c rmdir /s /q <dir>'. There's no macOS/Linux path, so --apply can't delete anything on the Mac mini. The fix must be SAFE: on non-Windows, lstat every entry and UNLINK symlinks rather than recursing into them, because overlay worktrees' node_modules are symlinks into the MAIN checkout, and a recursive delete through one would wipe the main checkout's files (the L-180 class). Pins: a temp tree with a symlink to an outside dir → the link is removed and the target is untouched. Owner: MacBook landing, stacked after #1318.
