## Reported evidence

ScanCamera.web.tsx's own adapter wiring -- the synchronous handleFrame call inside the decode .then(), the inFlightRef release in .finally(), the unconditional scanSettled drain, and the playScanCue call site -- has no coverage of its own: apps/mobile/**tests**/scan-engine.test.ts and scan-feedback.test.ts pin the pure lib/scan-engine.ts and lib/scan-feedback.ts logic those pieces call into, but mobile Jest never renders or exercises the component itself (pure-logic only, no camera/DOM harness). A refactor that re-nests the resolve call back inside the decode chain -- reintroducing the exact REG-B202 decode-starvation defect -- would keep every existing spec green.
