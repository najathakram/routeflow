## Reported evidence

The shared modal used across every platform-admin surface (tenant detail, feature console, override drawers) had no role=dialog/aria-modal, no Tab/Shift-Tab focus trap, no initial focus on open, and no focus restoration to the triggering element on close -- a keyboard/screen-reader user could tab out of an open modal into the page behind it, and focus was lost entirely on close.
