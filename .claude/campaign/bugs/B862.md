## Reported evidence

The Add User modal offers Role: Driver / Operator, but onSubmit never sends role, useCreateOperator also drops name, and createOperator hardcodes role OPERATOR. Anyone added as a 'Driver' here gets an office account with operator access instead; drivers must be created from Drivers → New Driver. The staff member's display name is also discarded.
