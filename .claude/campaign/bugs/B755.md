## Root cause

Found by the Opus review of #1271 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); pre-existing, verified on origin/master 2ed80789. The customer re-price effect deliberately leaves priceType DISCOUNTED/MANUAL lines alone so an operator's one-time price survives a customer change — but a line PREFILLED from customer A's order history arrives as DISCOUNTED/MANUAL too, so it inherits the exemption and keeps customer A's negotiated price when the order is switched to customer B. Money: the wrong customer's price is billed. Fix direction: distinguish history-prefilled prices (re-price, or re-prefill from B's history) from operator-entered ones (keep).
