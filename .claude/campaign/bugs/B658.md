## Reported evidence

The interactive create path charges the levy; the template path does not: same product, same customer, same levy, the tax depends on how the order was created. CHAIN: (1) createOrderFromTemplate (order-templates.service.ts ~437-506) writes trackedCategoryId: product.trackedCategoryId ?? null (:475) and categoryTaxAmount: 0 (:476) through order.create directly, not OrdersService.create() where the interactive path computes category tax; the order total is subtotal + roundMoney(subtotal x taxRate), regular tax only. (2) The only recompute is recomputeLineCategoryTaxes (orders.service.ts:230-272), called from exactly four places: the merge paths (:926, :1234), updateOrderItems (:4564) and the at-door change-request approval (:5543). The merge paths do not run for a lone generated order: mergeAllPendingForCustomer returns early when pendingOrders.length <= 1 (:640-642), and that is what generateOrder calls right after creating. The one recompute that could have saved it never fires. (3) An invoice built from the order copies the snapshot: buildInvoiceItemData prorates li.categoryTaxAmount (stored 0), and foldCategoryTax and the regulated-ledger SALE rows carry categoryTax: Number(it.categoryTaxAmount) = 0; no recompute was found at delivery or invoice creation. WHY IT IS DIFFERENT IN KIND: the 12x defects (B644-B657) are VISIBLE, drafts a human looks at. This one is INVISIBLE and accrues: category tax is silently under-collected on every standing-order order for a regulated product, flows into the invoice, and the ledger SALE row records categoryTax: 0. Nobody sees a wrong number; the regulated filing under-reports tax collected, a liability the business carries without knowing, discovered at audit. PRECONDITION: a customer with a VERIFIED license (unlicensed regulated lines are skipped by the template code) whose standing order contains a tracked-category product with a per-unit or percentage category levy. HOW KNOWN: code-read only, R-ui's own read, the three anchors (:476, :640-642, the four recompute call sites) confirmed by the Mac Lead and re-checked by the filer on origin/master; NEVER RUN. CROSS-REFERENCE: B647 (same file, same path, standing orders). MIRROR IMAGE: unverified, being chased (a new id may follow): manual-invoice-lines.ts pieceQty = boxes != null ? qty : upb > 1 ? qty * upb : qty may OVER-tax a boxed regulated qty-only line (recurring/duplicated); together the two would make the regulated tax basis wrong in both directions depending on the creation path. NO LEGAL CONCLUSION is drawn here: whether and how this affects a filing is a compliance question for the owner's advisor, recorded as a pair with the ledger soldAt question (which is NOT a bug id).
