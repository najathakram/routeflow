## Reported evidence

cancel()'s READ_ONLY short-circuit returned before the Stripe propagation block a later round had added below it, so a tenant the cron took READ_ONLY while its stripeSubId was still live kept being invoiced by Stripe, with no self-serve way out (web hides Cancel for READ_ONLY tenants). Fixed: tell Stripe first; for this cohort cancel IMMEDIATELY rather than at period end (lead ruling, owner-confirmed — the cohort already lost service at its last period end, so a period-end cancel bills a period of nothing).
