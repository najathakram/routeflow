# B814 · QuoteInlineReturnItemDto has no @IsDefined on boxes/pieces, so a bare-qty quote could bypass B774's pack-refusal guard (defense in depth; no live caller)

## Summary

Registry reconcile 2026-09-24 (Mac Lead, owner ask via the fleet lead): verified FIXED on origin/master by #1323. fix(returns,types): require boxes/pieces on inline returns, retire bare qty (SA-128, B814) (#1323) — makes boxes/pieces required on the return DTOs exactly per the bug's fix approach.

## Root cause

From the unit-picker lane's read-only hunt (2026-09-23); the fleet lead ruled to file. Verified on origin/master 361e46eb (2026-09-23). boxes?/pieces? are optional with no @IsDefined, and a bare qty is interpreted as selling units per returnRequestPieces (dto :19-23), so B774's mixed-pack refusal (inline-returns-quote.service.ts ~:244) could in principle be bypassed by a bare-qty request. The only caller (packages/returns inline-return-state.ts buildQuoteRequest) always sends both, and there's no mobile caller, so there's no live repro. Fix: make boxes/pieces required (or refuse a bare qty when the product's candidate lines span packs) with a DTO validation pin. Owner: Lane IR, when idle.

## Fix approach and UX

SA-128 (2026-09-23): OPTION (a). Lane IR's trace: the conflict and boxed-unknown-pack cases already refuse, and the residual is the axis GUESS a bare qty makes when 'never packed' and 'pack lost' look the same. Require boxes and pieces (@IsInt @Min(0), both required, at least one > 0; 0/0 → 400); qty optional and ignored when both are present; rewrite the DTO comment to explain the retirement; the SAME shape on the capture item DTO. Pins: {qty: 5} → 400 naming boxes/pieces; {0,5} with no pack → 5 pieces; {1,0} with no pack → 409 INLINE_RETURN_PACK_UNKNOWN; {0,0} → 400; the dialog's real request passes. Owner: Lane IR.
