## Reported evidence

Directly hit twice tonight: merging master or editing the lessons register invalidates .campaign/runs/{mobile,web}.json freshness even when that workspace's own tests didn't need to rerun, because turbo cache-skips the write. The freshness gate correctly refuses to trust the stale report (by design, per L-034), but there is no cheap way to re-stamp a report that is actually still accurate -- every push after a docs-only merge currently costs a full apps/api + apps/web + apps/mobile jest re-run just to satisfy the timestamp check.
