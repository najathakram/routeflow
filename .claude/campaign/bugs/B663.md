# B663 · A short or refused delivery closed on account leaves the invoice at the FULL ordered quantity: the delivered-basis reconcile never runs when the payment amount is 0

**Location** `apps/api/src/routes/routes.service.ts completeWithPayment (~2556-2625; guard :2607); apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts recordDeliveryPaymentInTx (early return :5079; reconcile calls :5126, :5135); apps/api/src/routes/routes.controller.ts (~200-209); apps/mobile short-pick.tsx (~143), payment.tsx (~332)` · **Severity** high · **State** uncampaigned

## Summary

UNITS-GATE CONDITION (fleet lead, from the Opus review of #1278, 2026-09-23): on-account stop close skips the delivered-basis reconcile (routes.service.ts ~:2607, payment branch only runs when dto.payment.amount > 0), so a line the driver marked 'Not delivered' under B648 stays billed. Must be fixed before flag.units_v1 goes to any non-test tenant.

Registry reconcile 2026-09-24 (Mac Lead, owner ask via the fleet lead): verified FIXED on origin/master by #1292. reconcileDeliveredOrdersInTx extracted and now runs unconditionally in completeWithPayment, before the payment-gated step — directly fixes the 'reconcile never runs when payment amount is 0' defect; landed after the note that flagged it.

## Fix approach and UX

REQUIRED SUB-ITEM OF THE FIX PR (I2): the customer's DELIVERED notification quotes the pre-short total from a pre-transaction snapshot (routes.service.ts:2697 orderTotal: formatMoney(o.total); the snapshot select at :2477; the other path at :2416 has the same shape, only grepped). It must quote the post-reconcile total. SIX PROOF CONDITIONS for the fix PR: (1) a short on-account delivery bills deliveredQty, and the levy on deliveredQty; (2) a REFUSED line bills 0; (3) the payment path (amount > 0) is unchanged; (4) an issued deposit mirror is rebuilt; (5) completing the same stop twice is idempotent; (6) the DELIVERED message quotes the post-reconcile total. The lines :2697, :2477 and :2416 were re-read by the filer on origin/master and match; the notification behaviour itself is code-read only, NEVER RUN.

## Reported evidence

THE DRIVER MAKES A COMMITMENT AND THE SOFTWARE BREAKS IT. The driver tells the customer 'short, see office invoice for final' (mobile short-pick.tsx ~143, sweeper claim) and the system then sends an invoice for the full ordered quantity, including refused lines: over-billing after promising a correction, to a customer told to expect one. Every other defect tonight is a machine being wrong in private; this one has a person making a commitment on our behalf that the software then breaks (the lead). WRONG AGAINST ITS OWN DOCUMENTED INTENT: routes.controller.ts ~203-207 says every mobile completion, including $0 'on account', flows through this endpoint 'for its delivered-basis invoice reconcile', and that completions with no payment (or amount 0) must keep working. Yet completeWithPayment (routes.service.ts ~2556-2625) calls recordDeliveryPaymentInTx only behind 'if (dto.payment && dto.payment.amount > 0)' (:2607), and recordDeliveryPaymentInTx itself returns immediately on '!(amount > 0)' (invoices.service.ts:5079). The delivered-basis rebuild (reconcileOrderDeliveredInvoices, :5135, or rebuildIssuedDepositMirrorOnDelivery for an issued deposit mirror, :5126) is called at exactly one place, inside recordDeliveryPaymentInTx; R-ui's grep of the whole API finds no other delivered-basis reconcile caller (the filer's grep of origin/master agrees: the only non-definition call sites are :5126 and :5135). completeWithPayment writes deliveredQty per line (REFUSED forces 0) and flips orders to DELIVERED via a direct order.updateMany, so changeStatus's DELIVERED logic does not run. The code's own comment says on-account sends amount 0 (:5081-5083); mobile sends no payment when the method is 'Account' or the tenant lacks the driver_payments addon (payment.tsx ~332, sweeper claim). EFFECT: the DRAFT mirror stays at the FULL ordered quantity including refused lines while the order reads DELIVERED and deliveredQty says less; it over-bills unless an office user edits the draft. MITIGATION: it is a DRAFT; no office screen was found that surfaces deliveredQty beyond a line-status badge (R-ui did not check). HOW KNOWN: R-ui verified, the Mac Lead re-confirmed the gating lines on origin/master (:2607, :5079, the controller comment), the filer re-read the same lines and the call-site list; code-read only, NEVER RUN. Flag OFF, ordinary lines, live today. FIX-SHAPE NOTE FOR THE RECORD (not a fix): the reconcile does not belong behind a payment guard at all; lifting it out so it runs on every completion is a contained restructure; recordDeliveryPaymentInTx does payment work and calling it with amount 0 is not obviously safe. PROD DATA QUESTION 13 (owner batch): how many tenants close deliveries on account with no payment, and how many of those had a short or refused line versus their delivered-basis invoice totals. Sweeper claims I1-I5 stay UNFILED (I2, the customer's DELIVERED notification quoting the pre-short total, is the one to re-read first). Severity HIGH as ruled by the lead.
