## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** A buyer scanning a barcode resolves it against the catalog their seller lets them see.

**Actually does.** Both product-resolve rungs are @Roles(OPERATOR, DRIVER) — every customer-role resolve 403'd, so the scan affordance could only appear to work against whatever stale product list the device had cached.

**The gap.** The scan feature shipped to the customer surface with no customer-permitted lookup behind it.
