## Reported evidence

Related to B894 (not the same fix). A free unit is a whole selling unit (a box) on a pieces axis, so no whole-free-unit allocation is monotone point by point: stored 24.00, 20 pcs, 2 free boxes of 4 - 9 pieces bills 18.00 but 10 pieces bills 12.00; a later partial delivery can be a NEGATIVE invoice line although the sum still equals the stored subtotal. Customer-visible on partial-delivery invoices. Fixing it needs free allocation in PIECES on invoice lines (promoFreeUnits axis, computeLineSubtotal contract, DRAFT round-trip, reconcile's sum-of-free-units mirror) and changes customer bills, so it is owner-visible: a plan and an owner yes come before any build.
