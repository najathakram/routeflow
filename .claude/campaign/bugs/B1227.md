## Reported evidence

On the counter, clicking Add note on a line leaves focus on the button instead of moving it into the note field. Typing the note then reaches the global g-prefix navigation shortcuts (e.g. g then o), the page navigates away and the in-progress cart is lost: the clerk loses a sale in progress. Found by UX manual check on master 39485bc52. Fix owner: PC lane.
