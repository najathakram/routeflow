## Reported evidence

recordStandalonePayment locks its invoices FOR UPDATE in id-sorted order while completeWithPayment locks them in delivery order; the resulting lock-order conflict can be detected by Postgres as a deadlock (40P01), failing the payment request outright with no automatic retry. (GitHub #2472)
