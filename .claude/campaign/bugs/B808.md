# B808 · Applying an advance payment never checks that the invoice belongs to the advance's customer, and the route ignores :id, so customer A's advance credit can be applied to customer B's invoice in the same tenant

## Root cause

Fleet lead relay, 2026-09-23. Verified on origin/master f9556ed2. customers.controller.ts:230-234 has @Post(':id/advance-payments/:apId/apply') applyAdvancePayment(@Param('apId') apId, @Body() dto: any), so the ':id' (customer) route param is never read and the body is untyped. customers.service.ts applyAdvancePaymentToInvoiceLocked (:1482) loads the advance by id (tx.advancePayment.findUnique, :1487) and the invoice by dto.invoiceId (tx.invoice.findUnique, :1504). It checks the advance's remaining balance, the invoice status and the outstanding balance, but NEVER compares inv.customerId to ap.customerId. An OPERATOR (or a crafted request) can therefore apply customer A's advance credit to customer B's invoice within the same tenant: A's prepaid money settles B's debt. MONEY plus intra-tenant authorization. Fix: refuse (400/403) when inv.customerId !== ap.customerId; bind and validate the route :id against ap.customerId; replace dto: any with a typed DTO (invoiceId @IsUUID). Red-first pins for the cross-customer apply, the :id mismatch and the happy path. customers.service.ts is AT its ratchet limit (3332 lines), so extract or stay net-0. Owner: the landing coordinator (it owns #1296 in the same function, and B310); it folds this into #1296's round if the SA agrees, else a separate PR right after.
