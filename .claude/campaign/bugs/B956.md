## Reported evidence

From SA-629 (#2554 5933736418), via Mac Lead. The regulated and tobacco CSV filings stay VERBATIM (no formula-injection guard, B953), so a customer, business or contact name starting with = + @ TAB or CR could execute as a formula when a filing is opened in a spreadsheet. Fix: API DTO validation on create and update refuses a leading = + @ TAB or CR in those name fields. Medium.
