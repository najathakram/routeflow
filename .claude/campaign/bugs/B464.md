## Reported evidence

(1) The platform-admin sidebar's responsive behavior breaks below roughly 640px viewport width. (2) The order-form shows no inline error when a manual discount exceeds the line/order total, only whatever generic validation catches it downstream. (3) Invoice total-invariant checks (total = subtotal - discount + shipping + tax) are re-derived ad-hoc at each consumer instead of one shared assertMoneyInvariants helper -- packages/pricing has no such export today, proposed to reduce drift risk across api/web/mobile.
