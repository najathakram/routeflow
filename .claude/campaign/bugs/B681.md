## Reported evidence

Pre-B661 SALE rows carried unitBasisQty as a box count for box-unaware regulated lines, not the piece count B661's fix now writes going forward. A piece-denominated return against one of those old rows over-closes the regulated ledger (the return's basis and the sale's basis disagree by the pack size). Needs the owner-run, dry-run-first, id-pinned correction script the Senior Architect designed (same B662 shape: unfiled periods only, never touches a filed/closed period).
