## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Scanning an archived product says so (“X is archived — reactivate to sell”); a parked draft keeps its lines.

**Actually does.** The two resolve rungs disagreed about isActive: the barcode rung found the product, the fallback search filtered it out — so the outcome depended on which rung answered, usually a false NOT FOUND. Draft-resume hard-dropped inactive lines, so a scan-added product could silently vanish when a draft was reopened.

**The gap.** Activity filtering lived in one rung and in the hydrator instead of being a first-class outcome the caller decides on.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/mobile/lib/barcode-resolve.ts + apps/web/lib/barcode-resolve.ts (mirror); barcode-resolve-archived.test.ts
