## Reported evidence

A commit reviewed in #2742 added 2,401 .jest-cache/ files (about 38 MB of source maps carrying a local absolute path) and nothing refused it: .jest-cache/ is not in .gitignore and the pre-push chain has no size or cache-dir check. The guard should (1) ignore .jest-cache/ and (2) refuse a push that adds files under cache or build directories, and any push adding more than N MB. Source: #2742 review, board #2554 comment 5884288517. Tooling.
