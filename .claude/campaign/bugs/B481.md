## Reported evidence

apps/web/app/buyer/layout.tsx's viewport export sets maximumScale:1 and userScalable:false, disabling pinch-to-zoom for every buyer-portal page. This fails WCAG 2.1 success criterion 1.4.4 (Resize Text) — a low-vision user on the buyer portal cannot zoom in at all, unlike every other shell (the root layout deliberately avoids this restriction).
