## Root cause

The bookkeeping ledger's Record Payment is a second, weaker payment writer that can resurrect a written-off invoice

Symptom / evidence: Recording a payment from the Ledger on a WRITTEN_OFF invoice flips it to PARTIAL/PAID with writeOffReason intact, mints no PAY- number, and takes no row lock.
