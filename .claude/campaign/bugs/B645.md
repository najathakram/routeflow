## Reported evidence

estimates.service.ts convertToInvoice creates the invoice line from only description, productId, qty, unitPrice, discount, taxRate, subtotal, msrp: it drops boxes, pieces and unitsPerBox. The invoice edit page hydrates isBoxSplit = it.boxes != null (false for the converted line) and sends {qty, unitPrice} without boxes/pieces, so the server update() prices per piece: an estimate line of 72 boxes of 12 ($20 per box, subtotal $1,440, qty 864 pieces) becomes $20 x 864 = $17,280 on Save; the form shows that total before Save. No spec pins boxes on convert. ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (shared by B641/B644/B645/B646): anywhere a line is reconstructed from {qty, unitPrice} without carrying boxes/pieces (and unitsPerBox), money or quantity is wrong by unitsPerBox. There are three independent reconstruction sites: Duplicate (B644), estimate convert (B645) and recurring invoices (B646), all predating units. All flag.units_v1 OFF, i.e. live today. Code-read by R-ui's sweep and verified again by the lead and the Mac Lead on origin/master; NEVER RUN.
