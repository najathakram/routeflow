## Root cause

PER-UNIT LEVY BASIS 12x ON SPLIT-LESS QTY-ONLY LINES (lead's ruling; no new id). The formula pieceQty = boxes != null ? qty : upb > 1 ? qty * upb : qty at apps/api/src/invoices/manual-invoice-lines.ts:88 (create) and apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts:3442 (update()) is the unitBasisQty for per-unit levies. computeCategoryTax (packages/pricing/src/pricing.ts:318-336) charges rate x unitBasisQty for EXCISE_PER_UNIT, PER_VOLUME and DEPOSIT_PER_CONTAINER; PERCENT_OF_SALE uses the subtotal. So a qty-only line on a boxed regulated product is OVER-taxed 12x. Worked example: upb 12, $0.10 per piece, 6 boxes at $40: correct levy $7.20; Duplicate/edit gives $86.40; a percentage levy is also 12x wherever the subtotal is wrong. CONDITION FOR THE FIX PR: it must assert the LEVY, not just the subtotal. A test that pins $240 and ignores $7.20 passes while the levy is still 12x wrong. See B659 for the four-paths framing. Code-read only, NEVER RUN.

## Reported evidence

estimates.service.ts convertToInvoice creates the invoice line from only description, productId, qty, unitPrice, discount, taxRate, subtotal, msrp: it drops boxes, pieces and unitsPerBox. The invoice edit page hydrates isBoxSplit = it.boxes != null (false for the converted line) and sends {qty, unitPrice} without boxes/pieces, so the server update() prices per piece: an estimate line of 72 boxes of 12 ($20 per box, subtotal $1,440, qty 864 pieces) becomes $20 x 864 = $17,280 on Save; the form shows that total before Save. No spec pins boxes on convert. ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (shared by B641/B644/B645/B646): anywhere a line is reconstructed from {qty, unitPrice} without carrying boxes/pieces (and unitsPerBox), money or quantity is wrong by unitsPerBox. There are three independent reconstruction sites: Duplicate (B644), estimate convert (B645) and recurring invoices (B646), all predating units. All flag.units_v1 OFF, i.e. live today.
