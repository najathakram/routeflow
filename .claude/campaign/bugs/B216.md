## Reported evidence

F18 made onSubscriptionDeleted/onSubscriptionUpdated clear downgradeToPlanKey/downgradeEffectiveAt/retainedUserIds, matching the one-armed-transition invariant subscription-mutation.service.ts now documents, but onPaymentSucceeded and onCheckoutCompleted (the reinstatement webhooks) do not: a tenant that lapses with a downgrade already armed keeps that stale schedule through reinstatement, so the next 02:00 sweep re-prices basePriceSnapshot against the pre-lapse schedule, books a second MRR delta, and can deactivate staff the reinstatement just restored. A safe fix needs the subscription's prior status available inside the same webhook transaction (F18 Option-B residual, not fixed).
