## Reported evidence

create(), DRAFT->PENDING promote, and settleStockForEdit all decrement a box-unaware line in BOXES against piece-denominated currentStock (orders.service.ts:1980, :2125, :2219-2227, :2695-2698; order-stock.ts:82-95, :164-200) -- never expanded to pieces at any sha since b8446f03. currentStock is pieces by definition (SA-060, all sixteen writers except this one). #1217's piece-basis restock is the CORRECT side of this pair (confirmed by SA-060, no revert) -- after #1217 this under-decrement becomes visible as a net stock gain on return instead of silently self-cancelling as it did before. Fix: sale-side decrement/restore must route through the same pack resolver #1217 uses. Existing shortfalls in currentStock are repaired only by a post-count append-only adjustment (SA-029), not a backfill script. High, fast-follow, release-blocking family closed by #1217 but this sibling is the reason the old numbers were already wrong under the covers.
