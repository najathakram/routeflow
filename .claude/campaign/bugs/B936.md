## Reported evidence

The driver change-requests POST has no server-side idempotency, so an offline replay of a queued mutation can create a second PENDING request. Follow-up to B923 (mobile api-client queues every failed mutation offline by default). Until fixed, entry 7 of the offline allow-list is online-only. Fix: server idempotency (key or natural-key dedupe) on the POST. Filed for N-kit via the lead.
