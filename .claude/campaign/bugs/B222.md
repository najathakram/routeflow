## Reported evidence

F08's B53 2+-creditable-invoice path computes headroom = Sigma(creditable invoice totals) - Sigma(non-VOID credit notes linked to the order) before minting against the LATEST creditable invoice, deliberately counting order-linked credit-note INTENT (OrderCreditNote) broadly so the mint-time guard cannot be tricked into over-refunding. credit-notes.service.create()'s own cumulative over-credit cap, by contrast, is scoped to the single source invoice being minted against. The two aggregates disagree on scope: an order with credit notes linked to OTHER creditable invoices on the same order can make the returns-side headroom check narrower than what create()'s own per-invoice cap would actually allow, so a legitimate refund can be refused by the mint-time guard even though create() would have accepted it. Needs a product ruling on what 'headroom' should mean across an order's credit-note graph before either side is changed.
