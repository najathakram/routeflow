## Summary

RE-SCOPED (2026-09-23, fleet lead ruling): the legacy Case/Unit toggle cannot take a per-unit price BY DESIGN, because it prorates linearly. Per-unit pricing IS the units_v1 feature (OrderLineUnitPicker.tsx + the product page's Units & prices card, ProductUnitsCard.tsx). ## Reported evidence

Owner-reported: cannot set a price per unit when selling by unit. A case at $12.59 for 15 units derives $0.84/unit, but the product actually sells at $1.29/unit, and there is no way to enter that. The money math for exactly this case is built, tested and live behind the units flag -- ProductUnit (catalog.prisma:313-341) carries an explicit price plus 4 tier prices per level, not just a conversion factor; resolveUnitPrice (packages/pricing/src/unit-levels.ts:186-203) already prefers an explicit level price and only falls back to division when one is absent; buildUnitLine (apps/orders/unit-line-build.ts:256-281) already accepts a staff-typed price and classifies it as a discount below list or an upsell above list. Only the surface is missing: the web Case/Unit toggle sends no unitLabel and the code comment states outright that the price is unchanged either way because proration is linear -- the toggle changes how a quantity reads, never what is charged. The single editable field remains the case price. See B737 for the missing unit-price-configuration screen this depends on.
