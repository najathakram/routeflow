applyPriceAdjustment rewrites unitPrice, subtotal and categoryTaxAmount on invoice items and never touches the ledger. Verified absence: R-ui read it, and the Mac Lead and the filer re-confirmed on origin/master that the function body (~6143-6388) has 0 references to ledger, writeSaleEntries or reverseInvoiceEntries; writeSaleEntries is called from exactly three places (invoices.service.ts:505 create, :1288 order to invoice, :1818 reconcile re-sync).
