## Reported evidence

Create a bill with a line of qty 10, edit it to qty 4: the bill carries both lines ("10,4") while totalOwed is computed from the new items only. Nested-created VendorBillItem rows have NULL tenantId, so the tenant-scoped deleteMany misses them. Fix: delete via the parent nested deleteMany (as revertToDraft does) AND stamp tenantId on the nested creates; the SA rules the platform-wide pattern and audits other nested creates. Money.
