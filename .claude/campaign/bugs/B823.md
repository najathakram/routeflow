## Root cause

Found in the phone-fix Playwright proof (the unit-picker lane, 2026-09-23; commit 927d514e against the local compose web, test tenant); confirmed by the Mac Lead reading local-assets/proofs/2026-09-23/mobile-web-proxy/android-390-orders.png against android-768-orders.png. Now that phones get the redesigned web (the owner ruling), /orders at 390: (1) the orders TABLE renders its desktop layout, so the TOTAL column's amounts are cut off at the right edge ('$12.0…'); page scrollWidth is clean, so it's an inner horizontal scroll with no visible affordance; (2) the status filter chip row is clipped ('Out for Delive…'), which may be an intended horizontal-scroll chip row but has no affordance; (3) the '6 parked drafts' dock plus its two expanded DRAFT cards float OVER the first order rows, hiding them. No data is lost, but the primary operator list isn't usable at a phone width. Fix: a mobile card layout for the orders list below md (mirror the redesign's list-card pattern), a visible scroll affordance on the chip row, and a dock that collapses or docks below the list at small widths. Proof: Playwright at 390 on the same pages. Owner: unassigned; the fleet lead routes it.
