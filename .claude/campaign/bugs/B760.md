# B760 · createPartialFromOrder computes remaining as qty - invoicedQty with no delivered guard, so 'invoice remaining' on a DELIVERED order can bill refused or short-delivered goods

## Root cause

From SA-093 (#1274 round 5; fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); verified on origin/master 2ed80789: createPartialFromOrder's per-item validation uses remaining = Number(li.qty) - Number(li.invoicedQty ?? 0) (:2702) and createInvoiceFromOrder builds remainingQty the same way (:585) — neither caps by deliveredQty. On a DELIVERED order where the driver recorded a refusal or a short delivery, the office's 'invoice remaining' can bill goods the customer never received. SA-093 rules the delivery is the authority on what was sold. Fix: remaining = min(qty, deliveredQty) - invoicedQty for a DELIVERED order (undelivered orders keep qty - invoicedQty); check the :585 sibling in the same PR. Owner: Lane F, next after #1274. Money — carve-out parked.

Restored 2026-09-24 (text overwritten in 2d69b8b9 by a note --section replace): DEFINITION (SA-106, fleet lead relay 2026-09-23): on a DELIVERED order, remaining = min(qty, delivered basis) − invoicedQty, where the delivered basis is SA-093a's evidence-keyed one (DeliveryMutation rows per line; no mutation for a line → its ordered qty; no mutations on the order → undelivered behaviour) via the helper shared with #1274. A partial invoice request beyond that remaining is REFUSED, and the error names the delivered qty. Sequenced after #1292 (SA-106: B760 next), with #1274 and B785.

Restored 2026-09-24 (text overwritten in 491e432b by a note --section replace): BASIS CORRECTED (SA-093a, 2026-09-23): do NOT cap by deliveredQty — only completeWithPayment stamps it, so office-delivered and completeStop orders would read 0 delivered. The delivered basis is evidence-keyed on DeliveryMutation rows per line (no mutation for a line → its ordered qty; no mutations on the order → undelivered behaviour), via ONE helper shared with #1274 (B667). Use that helper here; remaining = basis − invoicedQty.
