## Reported evidence

C-2 holds that each of the 15 converted cron methods makes exactly one forSystem call with its allow-list reason, and L-1 (outsideForSystem) reports any this.<member> access, any this that is not a member-access receiver, a non-function second argument and a first statement that is not the forSystem call, outside the callback. The lead named two shapes the pin does not cover (recorded here as the known limits, not yet pinned): (1) a module-level or imported helper that is passed a client (the helper is outside the wrapper and the pin only reads this.<member> and this escapes); (2) an inline callback that reaches out of the wrapper. Both reviews (A and B) rated the pin-shape findings LOW on #4229 (merge ce0ed80a11, head 88c2f2c2fd), and the guard is dark. Suggested direction, not decided: a type-level answer (the job body receives the scoped client and holds no PrismaService reference) before the enforce flip, rather than more pattern pins.
