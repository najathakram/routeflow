## Reported evidence

resolvePOLine (PO line create, U step 3) stores the per-piece unit cost rounded to 4 decimal places instead of carrying the box cost + pack size (or a higher-precision fraction) through to the total. $11/box / 12 units = $0.9167 (4dp), and 1,000 boxes at that rounded per-piece rate totals $11,000.40 on both the PO total and inventory value -- a real (if small-per-line) money drift that compounds at scale. Exposed by #1165's read-back (a 11.0004 per-box cost read-back). Senior Architect is ruling on the fix shape; carve-out: touches money, plan only, do not fix unattended.
