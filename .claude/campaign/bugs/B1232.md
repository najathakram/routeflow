## Reported evidence

CreateProductDto and UpdateProductDto validate costingMethod with IsEnum(CostingMethod), so PATCH/POST with FIFO or LIFO returns 200. Owner ruling 2026-10-16 (SA-804 1, Q-L3) retires FIFO/LIFO: the validators must refuse them with 400 COSTING_METHOD_RETIRED. Red: apps/api/src/inventory/costing-method-retired.spec.ts (SA-804 a).
