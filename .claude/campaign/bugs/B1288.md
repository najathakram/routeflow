## Reported evidence

executeBulk sends sequential PATCHes; the catch only sets bulkError, and fetchTenants, the deselect and the close run only after a full success. After a mid-list refusal the list is stale, nothing says how many businesses moved, and a re-run re-sends the ones already moved. A mid-list refusal is ordinary, not exotic: updatePlanLocked (apps/api/src/platform-admin/platform-admin.service.ts:951-1040) refuses a business with a pending cancellation (400) and assertTenantNotPaymentOverdue refuses an overdue one (409), so any mixed selection hits it. A same-plan re-send is not short-circuited (it falls to the instant-apply path), so a retry adds audit rows. bulkError also shows the raw server message. Suspend and activate share the loop. Fix: per-id results, refetch in finally, deselect the successes, show "N of M moved" and name the refused ones. Source: bug hunt 2026-11-06, verify-ui C-4 (confirmed, read at 7be372afed).
