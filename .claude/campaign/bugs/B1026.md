## Reported evidence

From the lead (the #3104 Opus review). create() refuses a negative line subtotal with a 400 through assertNoNegativeLineSubtotal but update() never calls it. Example: 1 x $5 with a $50 discount stores -45 and lowers the invoice total. Fix: call the same guard in update(). Money, api, high.
