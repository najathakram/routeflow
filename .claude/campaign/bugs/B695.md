## Reported evidence

reopenOrder (orders.service.ts:3007-3063) has no compare-and-swap: the updateMany result is ignored (:3040-3043), then a relative stock decrement follows unconditionally. Concurrent reopens of the same order double-decrement stock. Sibling transitions at :2674-2682 and :2715-2723 already use CAS; this one was missed.
