## Reported evidence

From the MacBook's B965 read of master via Mac Lead (severity TBD by the SA; Mac Lead suggests medium money). The B947 (#2933) order pool's credit-note take side (pooledTakeRefusal) and credit-note create() work in PIECES (x piecesPerQty), and inline returns store pieces. A STANDARD return stores ReturnItem.qty in the ORDER LINE's own unit (B665), and the pool's refund pre-check (pooledRefundRefusal -> planProductRefund) compares that line-unit qty against line-unit pool lines, so the same pool mixes units across its refund and credit-note sides. It only affects products billed on lines of different units; single-unit products are fine. Related to B965 (now HIGH). Money, medium (SA to rule).
