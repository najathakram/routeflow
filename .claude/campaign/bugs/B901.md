## Reported evidence

iOS: the price-edit modal (LinePriceModal / Edit price) never appears from the scan tray while the scan sheet is open; it queues behind ScanOrderSheet's Modal and shows only after Done. Pre-existing on master 2a27877f; affects standard and special lines. Repro: New order, pick a customer, add a line, open the scan sheet (camera denied is fine), press the line's price pencil. It makes #2751's special-price override unreachable from the scan tray on iOS. The fix is owner-ruled: old native screen versus the hybrid shell. Source: U-units, board #2554 comment 5893110537.
