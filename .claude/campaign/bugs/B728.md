## Reported evidence

packages/types/index.ts:17-23 exports a hand-written OrderStatus enum with five values, missing DRAFT and PARTIALLY_DELIVERED, while the Prisma enum (sales.prisma:4-12) has seven. Web and mobile each declare their own correct seven-value local union instead of importing the shared one, so nothing breaks today -- but it is exactly the hand-typed server-enum mirror shape that lesson L-072 says shipped three real bugs. Not in packages/types/api/enums.ts where the enum-parity spec would have caught it. Fast-follow.
