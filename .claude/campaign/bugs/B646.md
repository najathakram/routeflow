## Reported evidence

recurring-invoices.service.ts passes {description, productId, qty, unitPrice, discount, taxRate} to invoicesService.create() with no boxes/pieces. The operator types boxes against a box price, so the line stores qty 6 (boxes) in the pieces column where the true quantity is 72. R-ui verified estimateCogs (common/invoiced-sales.ts ~433-450) = sum of line.qty x per-piece cost, so COGS is understated 12x and profit overstated. The billed money is right by intent, which is why it has not been noticed. Sales-by-item, analytics and the regulated ledger are listed as readers of that column but were NOT verified. ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (shared by B641/B644/B645/B646): anywhere a line is reconstructed from {qty, unitPrice} without carrying boxes/pieces (and unitsPerBox), money or quantity is wrong by unitsPerBox. There are three independent reconstruction sites: Duplicate (B644), estimate convert (B645) and recurring invoices (B646), all predating units. All flag.units_v1 OFF, i.e. live today. Code-read by R-ui's sweep and verified again by the lead and the Mac Lead on origin/master; NEVER RUN.
