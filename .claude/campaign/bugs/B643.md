## Reported evidence

Read-only from code on tree a6b3910a (master 6844c295 + #982 + #1121); NEVER SEEN RUNNING. apps/mobile/app/(driver)/route/stop/[stopId]/short-pick.tsx is an integer stepper in PIECES (its own comment: orderedQty and the qty steppers below are in PIECES), shows Ordered {qty} / Delivered [n], is capped at Number(li.qty), and has no unitLabel/boxLabel handling at all. routes/delivery-lines.util.ts accepts a delivered quantity anywhere in [0, ordered qty] with no unit awareness. Consequence: for a line sold as 3 Cases (qty 36 pieces) a driver who delivers 3 Cases and types 3 records 3 pieces instead of 36, a 12x under-delivery entered in good faith, feeding the delivery progress line and plausibly invoicing and returns. Exposure is zero while flag.units_v1 is off; it gates a real-tenant grant. Found by the Mac F2 prover while fixturing the #1121 proof (state C3'). Filed as a bug entry only, no fix.
