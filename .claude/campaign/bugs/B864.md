## Reported evidence

When planKey is set and basePriceSnapshot is null, updateTenantPriceOverride backfills the snapshot AND emits PLAN_CHANGED +monthly without the paying-set check (isPayingRow), so a TRIAL tenant is booked into the ledger before it pays. Local run: +1,200 ledger on a TRIAL Enterprise tenant with MRR unchanged; pinned by REG-OVERRIDE-BACKFILL-TRIAL.
