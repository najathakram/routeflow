## Reported evidence

POST /estimates with taxAmount:-100 on a $20-subtotal estimate persists total:-80; taxAmount:9999 persists total:10019; discount:1000 persists total:-980. Independently GET /estimates/{id} confirms each is durable. Estimates convert to invoices via POST /estimates/{id}/convert-to-invoice, copying est.subtotal/est.taxAmount verbatim, so a fabricated total propagates into real AR.
