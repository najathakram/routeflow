## Root cause

From the MacBook lane's B673 work (its S3 ruled it out of that PR as a different money path); fleet lead relay 2026-09-23. Re-verified by the Mac Lead on origin/master d007dfec: approveChangeRequestAtStop's ADD_ITEM branch sets addQty = Number(payload.qty) (:5379), never reads payload.boxes/pieces, and merges into a held same-product line as newQty = Number(existing.qty) + addQty (:5399); it re-splits with normalizeBoxesPieces only when the existing line already has boxes (wasBoxSplit), otherwise keeps a bare qty — i.e. it assumes the existing line's axis. (a) A legacy box-unaware existing line (qty 1 box, boxes null, upb 12, $40) plus a driver {qty:12, boxes:1, pieces:0} gives bare qty 13 → billed $520 where $80 is correct ($440 OVERcharge). (b) A box-split existing line (1 box, qty 12) plus a buyer's bare qty:1 meaning one box gives 13 pieces → billed 1 box + 1 piece, $43.33 where $80 is correct ($36.67 UNDERcharge). Sibling: the at-stop NEW-line path silently re-splits against the LIVE pack after a pack change, re-pricing without consent, while next-delivery now refuses — owner policy call: at-stop should adopt the refusal. The B673 PR records (a) and (b) as test.failing characterisations tagged B753.

## Fix approach and UX

FIX SHAPE SUPERSEDED BY SA-104 (2026-09-23) — do NOT classify-and-promote. The order-line axis rule is B655's, not the ledger classifier. The at-stop merge must NEVER promote a legacy line by writing boxes and a pack onto it (that re-denominates qty and its siblings invoicedQty/deliveredQty and flips the ledger classifier on box-axis rows). The EXISTING line's denomination wins: convert the add into it — whole boxes only into a box-unaware line; loose pieces refuse with 'approve as a new line'; priced at the line's agreed unitPrice with BOGO rescaled. A pack mismatch refuses for BOTH the new line and the merge, naming both packs and the remedy. Extract to at-stop-add-merge.ts; sequenced after #1286 and after B776, reusing B776's single pack-resolution helper. Owner: the MacBook lane.
