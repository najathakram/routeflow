## Reported evidence

From the #2933 Opus r4 review (N3, #2554 verdict-2933-r4), via the lead. The standard-return refund door adds quantities across invoice lines that use different units (boxes and pieces) in its qty headroom. Pre-existing. The money cap still binds, so the effect is a false refusal or an inaccurate qty check, not an over-credit. Fix: convert to pieces (divide by piecesPerQty) like #2933's pool.
