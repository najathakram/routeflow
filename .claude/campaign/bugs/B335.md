## Root cause

CSV importers' date try/catch blocks are dead code, so an unparseable date kills the row (and aborts the whole expense import)

Symptom / evidence: A day-first date like "31/07/2026" yields Invalid Date instead of the documented fallback: invoice/payment rows fail with a serialisation error, and one bad Expense Date aborts importExpenses entirely.
