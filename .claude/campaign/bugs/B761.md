## Root cause

From SA-093 ruling (B) (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23): #1274 runs the invoicedQty recompute in its OWN transaction under an order-keyed advisory lock (db-locks.ts), after the edit's transaction commits — the commit-to-recompute window is this follow-up. Fix: thread the caller's tx into the recompute from its three callers (invoices.service.ts :3522, :3583, :6343 — line numbers on #1274's branch at f644debd, not on master, where the recompute does not exist yet). Deadlock checks the lead attached to #1274 carry over: no caller may hold an uncommitted tx on the same rows while calling a recompute that opens its own, and no order-lock -> customer-lock inversion. Depends on #1274 landing.
