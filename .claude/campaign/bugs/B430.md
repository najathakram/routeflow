## Reported evidence

Verified against master d69f3094: mrr.service.ts's ledgerAgg = this.prisma.billingEvent.aggregate({ _sum: { amountDelta: true } }) and momAgg (same aggregate, createdAt >= monthAgo) have NO where-clause filtering by tenant status or deletedAt -- unlike every other query in the same Promise.all (subs/addons/trialTenants/readOnlyTenants all filter deletedAt: null). deleteTenant (platform-admin.service.ts:340) sets deletedAt + status:CANCELLED but never writes a BillingEvent/amountDelta row to offset the tenant's prior positive contribution. Net effect: ledgerMrr and momDelta permanently overstate MRR by the sum of every deleted tenant's historical billing events, forever, with no correction mechanism. #743 review finding F5. Fix: scope both aggregates to non-deleted tenants (a join/filter on tenant.deletedAt), or book an offsetting negative delta in deleteTenant.
