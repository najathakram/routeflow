## Reported evidence

Two rebuilds of the same draft (two staff marking delivered, a delivery racing an edit, or POST /invoices/from-order) interleave deleteMany and create outside a transaction, doubling lines while the stored total stays single. Fix shape: one tx locking the order then the draft (same order update() uses), re-reading under the lock. Verified refute-first; live for every tenant.
