## Reported evidence

apps/web/app/(marketing)/marketing.css is a ~10,092-line .rf-marketing-scoped stylesheet (tokens, utilities, per-page rules) ported near-verbatim from the redesign and loaded once from app/layout.tsx, deliberately kept separate from apps/web/app/globals.css and packages/config/tailwind.config.ts -- both pinned byte-identical to the pre-port baseline by marketing-port.static.test.ts's MKT-PIN T7 guards (app/globals.css is byte-identical to the branch baseline; packages/config/tailwind.config.ts is byte-identical to the branch baseline). Two parallel styling systems now ship side by side with no shared token layer; consolidating marketing's tokens into the app's own Tailwind/CSS-variable system is deferred until the redesign migration finishes elsewhere -- MKT-PIN's own stated purpose is to keep this red gate closed in the meantime, not to be the permanent shape.
