## Reported evidence

The feature registry declares driver_payments requires anyOf [recurring_routes, order_delivery], but nothing enforces that dependency client-side: the legacy toggle card and the new Feature Console 'Enable as add-on' modal both POST /platform-admin/tenants/:id/addons/enable for driver_payments unconditionally, with no check for whether the tenant already holds one of its two prerequisite addons. A super-admin can enable driver_payments alone, leaving the tenant billed/flagged for at-door payment collection with no route surface (Dispatch or Deliveries) where a driver would ever reach the gated endpoint (POST /route-runs/:id/stops/:stopId/complete-with-payment). Pre-existing behavior, carried forward unchanged by B2b (PR #872) rather than introduced by it -- found during that ticket's B2b/ticket-4 analysis, filed per lead allocation.
