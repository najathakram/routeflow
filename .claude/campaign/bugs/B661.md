## Reported evidence

THE LEVERAGE POINT (the lead's words): writeSaleEntries sets qty = it.qty and unitBasisQty = it.qty (regulated-ledger.service.ts ~76-79) with its own comment 'W5: no unitBasis conversion yet (W3 owns pack/carton math) — passthrough'. It is THE SINGLE PLACE a quantity should be converted into the filing's unit basis; because it is not, every creation-path variation leaks straight into the filing CSVs and tobacco screens (readers: regulated.service.ts:46, regulated-filing.service.ts:125-126, filing-csv.ts:104-170, tobacco-report.service.ts:211, tobacco.service.ts:69,167; the readers are sweeper claims, the writer was confirmed by R-ui and re-read by the Mac Lead). A 6-box line books qty 6 while the levy is on 72 pieces: units 12x low, dollars right. The TX report compensates only when regUomCase is set (tx-report.ts:295-301; its comment says not to 'fix' it). FIXING THE CREATION PATHS stops the leak for NEW lines; FIXING THE CONVERTER makes the reports right REGARDLESS OF PATH, including every line already stored. Related: B658, B659, B645, B656, B647 (creation-path variations), B660, B662. NOT folded into B660 or B662 (lead ruling). HOW KNOWN: code-read only; NEVER RUN.
