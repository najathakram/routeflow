## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Impersonating a tenant presents that tenant&rsquo;s workspace: its branding in the sidebar, and the acting identity in the header, matching the red banner and the data on screen.

**Actually does.** Only the banner and the data followed the switch. AuthProvider (header identity) and TenantProvider (logo + business name) snapshot their state once at mount and had no subscriber to the impersonation-change event &mdash; the banner was the sole listener. The tenants-list Impersonate button also used a soft router.push and never wrote the tenant-slug cookie, unlike the tenant-detail button which did a full document load.

**The gap.** A super-admin saw one tenant&rsquo;s branding and username over another tenant&rsquo;s data &mdash; the exact confusion an impersonation banner exists to prevent. Exiting had the mirror problem: a soft navigation that restored nothing, leaving the impersonated tenant&rsquo;s identity on the admin&rsquo;s own session.

**Evidence.** Evidence · Reported live: impersonating acme-distribution from the tenants list served that tenant&rsquo;s drivers while the sidebar read &ldquo;RouteFlow Demo&rdquo; and the header chip read routeflow_demo. apps/web/lib/auth-context.tsx (mount-only getStoredUser()); apps/web/components/tenant-provider.tsx (mount-only branding fetch, focus/visibility recheck only); apps/web/lib/impersonation.ts (subscribeImpersonation had a single caller, the banner); apps/web/app/(platform-admin)/admin/tenants/page.tsx (soft nav, no setTenantCookie). Found in the same trace: parseExp called atob on a raw base64url segment, so it threw on any token containing - or _ and expired was pinned false forever &mdash; the expired-impersonation banner and the proactive expiry clear were dead code.
