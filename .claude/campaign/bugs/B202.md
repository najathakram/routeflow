## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** B190&ndash;B201 were reported by an operator scanning on a phone browser. Fixing them should have fixed their scanner.

**Actually does.** #555 rewrote the native camera and deliberately left ScanCamera.web.tsx on the old engine — so the person who reported the loss kept every symptom. It stacked three faults: the decode loop held its in-flight guard across the whole server lookup, so no frame was even decoded while a scan resolved; fire() then bailed on a busyRef boolean before the gate, discarding anything that did arrive with zero feedback (B192 verbatim); and its finally re-anchored the gate to the legacy single-slot shape, which also made the two-code-carton runaway (B190 class) reachable for locally-cached products. Separately, mobile web had no accept feedback of any kind — lib/haptics.ts's scanHaptic returns early on web — so an operator watching the goods rather than the screen had nothing to tell them a scan landed.

**The gap.** A platform variant excluded from the batch that fixed its own logic, on the one platform the reporter actually uses.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/mobile/components/ScanCamera.web.tsx (fire() busyRef guard ahead of gateScan; the rAF loop's detect().then(fire) holding inFlightRef; the finally-block gateRef re-anchor) vs apps/mobile/components/ScanCamera.tsx as shipped by #555; apps/mobile/lib/haptics.ts (web early-return); REG-B202
