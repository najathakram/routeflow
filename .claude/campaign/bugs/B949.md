## Reported evidence

SA-608i (#2554 5928571780). When an invoice is voided, reversing its application of a credit note whose source invoice was deleted (status SOURCE_DELETED) puts that credit back as spendable: an orphan credit with no live source. Fix shape (SA): refuse with 409 CREDIT_NOTE_SOURCE_GONE when voidInvoice would reverse an application of a SOURCE_DELETED note, never restore it; plus a count-only sizing query (read-only, per SA post).
