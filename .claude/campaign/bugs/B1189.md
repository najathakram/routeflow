## Reported evidence

reserveReturnNo reserves the return/credit-note number outside the create transaction; when the create then loses a unique-key race, the reserved number is never used, leaving a gap in the sequence (a gap, not a duplicate). Accepted behaviour per SA-717a; documented at reserveReturnNo and in #3257.
