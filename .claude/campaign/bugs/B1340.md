## Reported evidence

SA-batch-0751 section 4 (W-965): every regulated ledger writer buckets by UTC (periodBucketOf), while whether a period has ended is judged on the tenant's clock, so a lock can be taken once the tenant's month is over. For a tenant east of UTC, in the hours between its local month end and UTC's, any regulated write into that month (a routed late sale and an ordinary current sale alike) is refused 409 by the guard: fail closed, not money-unsafe, not a routing defect. No current tenant is east of UTC. Moving buckets to the tenant's zone changes what periodBucket means ledger-wide (M, with historic rows), so it is not a W-965 fix. Stated as a known limit in the owner box; does not block the flip.
