# B1400 · A checkout completion stamps billingInterval but never sets the subscription cycle, so a year checkout keeps the MONTHLY default

**Location** `apps/api/src/billing/billing.service.ts checkout.session.completed upsert (:2288, :2294)` · **Severity** high · **Batch** F65 · **State** queued

## Summary

_Not yet analysed._

## What this feature is for

_Not yet analysed._

## Root cause

One upsert stamps the interval and forgets the cycle in both arms.

## User impact

_Not yet analysed._

## Fix approach and UX

BP-2 (SA-1304 R-1/W-0 ruling): the webhook upsert sets cycle from the stamped interval for every checkout session, in billing.service.ts only. A hard prerequisite of PR-3 together with a reconcile of any such rows (Amendment 1 A9).

## Test plan

T1. REG-B1400-checkout-cycle-from-interval: complete a session with metadata.interval year on a test tenant; cycle and interval agree.

## Reported evidence

The only writer of billingInterval is the checkout.session.completed upsert and it never writes cycle, so a session completed with interval year leaves cycle MONTHLY with billingInterval year. The catch-up checkout completes through the same upsert.
