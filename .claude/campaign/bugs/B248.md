## Reported evidence

marketing.css declares --plum: #7b49ab once (:39, also reused as --ring) but does not consistently read it back via var(--plum): the same hex is hardcoded again as a color at :9591, :9601 and :9711. A second, visually-adjacent purple, #623691, is hardcoded as a card background at :9486 and :9682 (.capability-7) with no token of its own, alongside a small untokenised family of near-plum hexes (#dcc1ef/#6d369e/#c7a2e1/#714a8b, :9478-9483) on sibling capability cards. One shared plum/purple token family would remove five of these six hardcoded values.
