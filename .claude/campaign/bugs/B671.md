## Reported evidence

returnedPiecesByProduct adds STANDARD ReturnItem.qty (line-unit, ~126-129) and INLINE ReturnItem.qty (true pieces, ~131-133) into one result[productId], and the sum feeds the return cap at returns.service.ts:222 and inline-returns-quote.service.ts:187. Repro: a box-unaware line of 6 BOXES of a 12-pack (qty 6). A STANDARD return of 6 (all of it), then an INLINE return of 12 pieces, gives alreadyReturned = 18; either order hits 18 against a cap of either 6 (line unit) or 72 (pieces), so a whole return is wrongly refused or wrongly allowed. Fix: convert the STANDARD side to pieces through the shared line-pack-factors.ts / legacyLinePieces before summing. Found by Lane B. Owner: U-units, fixed with the classifier.
