## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Merging an incoming order into the open one folds denominations losslessly under a lock, and keeps an operator's price override.

**Actually does.** The merge flat-summed quantities (2 boxes + 5 pieces became 7 BOXES — an overcharge by unitsPerBox, the exact class the money discipline bans), and computed from a controller-side pre-read outside any lock, so concurrent merges clobbered each other's lines.

**The gap.** Denomination-blind arithmetic on boxed lines, outside the transaction that owns the order.
