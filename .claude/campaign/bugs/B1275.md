## Reported evidence

SA-827 snapshots the invoice display unit once at DRAFT to issued, and a revert to draft clears it (clearIssueDisplayUnits, called only in the markSent revert at invoices.service.ts:3959). The other three writes that set status to DRAFT (order-edit revert ~4029, unvoid ~4064, reopen of a PAID invoice ~4088) do not clear it, so the invoice keeps the unit label and factor frozen at its earlier issue: a DRAFT does not render live, and a re-issue may not freeze afresh. Display of a money document only; totals are not changed. Reported by SA, verified on origin/master at 17:09Z. Anchor SA-1029 A-1. Filed only; no fix card (over the PR limit).
