## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** Selecting a date range on Analytics should scope every card on the page — including DSO, Route Performance, Driver Performance, Dead Stock, and Margin Alerts — to that period.

**Actually does.** The page sends {from,to} to all five endpoints, but the controller methods for dso/routes-performance/drivers-performance/margin-alerts take no query params at all, and dead-stock only reads a daysInactive param (default 30) — services compute all-time (or rolling-30-day) aggregates.

**The gap.** Changing the date picker visibly re-fetches these cards but their numbers never change — DSO/route/driver/margin cards are unbounded all-time, dead-stock uses a fixed 30-day-from-now cutoff, not the picker's range.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/web/app/(dashboard)/analytics/page.tsx:316-323(dso),546-561(deadStock,marginAlerts),845-861(routes,drivers) all pass {from,to}; apps/api/src/analytics/analytics.controller.ts:69-91,94-97 (getRoutes/getDrivers/getMarginAlerts/getDso take zero @Query args, getDeadStock only @Query('daysInactive')); apps/api/src/analytics/analytics.service.ts:266(getRoutePerformance),289(getDriverPerformance),359(getDeadStock(daysInactive=30)),428(getMarginAlerts),452(getDso) — none accept from/to

**Suggested fix (register).** Add from/to @Query params to the routes/performance, drivers/performance, dso, and margin-alerts controller methods and thread them into the corresponding service queries (filter RouteRun by date, PAID invoices by paidAt/issueDate window, products' margin snapshot isn't inherently dated so consider a point-in-time note); replace dead-stock's daysInactive with the picker's from/to.

> ⚠️ Treat this as a hypothesis, not a plan. On F11 the adversarial pass refuted the
> suggested fix for every one of the four bugs while confirming every diagnosis.
