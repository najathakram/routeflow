## Reported evidence

Partial invoicing accepts a piece qty on a Case line: the money is prorated correctly, but the invoice line keeps unitLabel Case with boxes 0 / pieces N, breaking the boxes = count, pieces = 0 storage rule; the PDF then prints "8 boxes + 4 pcs". HOW KNOWN: sweeper x2 (two independent sweepers agree); NOT verified by R-ui. ROOT CAUSE (shared with B641/B643/B644/B645/B646): computeLineSubtotal and normalizeBoxesPieces silently fall back to per-piece math when a caller omits the split or the factor, and no client type (web, mobile, @routeflow/types) carries unitLabel, so no client can be unit-aware; the fence (assertNoUnitAwareLines etc.) covers rewrite paths only, not client-typed quantities or documents. Cross-reference B643 (driver stepper, sweep B1: do not refile). Source: R-ui's unitLabel sweep (local-assets/proofs/2026-09-21/unitlabel-sweep.md, section B, row B6), read against a source-only snapshot of origin/master c9200fc2, NOT the newest master.
