## Reported evidence

needsMarginAck compares the cent-rounded price against priceForMarginFloor (cost / (1 - floor) rounded to the NEAREST cent via roundMoney, packages/pricing/src/pricing.ts ~:253), while the list row's badge and the picker strip's label classify by the exact fraction (computeMarginFraction -> classifyMargin). At the boundary the two can disagree by up to half a cent in either direction, so a line can show 'Below floor' with no ack asked, or be asked for an ack while classified ok. Fix approach: one basis -- needsMarginAck decides via classifyMargin(computeMarginFraction(price, cost, unitsPerBox), floor) evaluating to other than 'ok' (or priceForMarginFloor rounds UP); pin with a pure test at the boundary (cost 7.00, floor 0.15 -> floor price 8.235 rounds to 8.24; price 8.24 vs 8.23). Found by the #675 final pass (fix-round-3-light.md).
