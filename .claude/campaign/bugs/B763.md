# B763 · Bulk/standalone payment saves its unallocated leftover as a new AdvancePayment without roundMoney (breaks 'round every monetary write')

**Location** `apps/api/src/invoices/invoices.service.ts:5776-5781 (recordStandalonePayment); check sibling :5268 (recordDeliveryPaymentInTx)` · **Severity** medium · **State** uncampaigned

## Summary

Registry reconcile 2026-09-24 (Mac Lead, owner ask via the fleet lead): verified FIXED on origin/master by #1287. Both AdvancePayment excess writes (recordStandalonePayment, recordDeliveryPaymentInTx) now wrap excess in roundMoney, with a red-first float-residue proof.

## Root cause

From the Opus review of #1276 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); pre-existing, verified on origin/master 2ed80789: recordStandalonePayment computes `const excess = dto.totalAmount - allocatedTotal` (:5776) from float sums and writes `advancePayment.create({ amount: excess })` (:5781) with no roundMoney, so a sub-cent float residue can be persisted as a customer's advance balance — CLAUDE.md money discipline: round every monetary write. Sibling to check in the same PR: recordDeliveryPaymentInTx writes `amount: excess` (:5268) where excess = remaining (:5245) — confirm remaining is rounded there too. Fix: roundMoney(excess) at the write (and keep the > 0.001 threshold on the rounded value).

SCOPE CORRECTED (Lane F's proof, fleet lead relay 2026-09-23): the sibling recordDeliveryPaymentInTx (`amount: excess`, ~:5244/:5268) is NOT a live defect — that method rounds `remaining` at every step (initial roundMoney(amount); applyHere = roundMoney(min(...)); remaining = roundMoney(remaining - applyHere)), so excess = remaining is always clean for reachable inputs (Lane F: exhaustive cent sweep + 200,000 randomized multi-invoice chains, zero drift). The LIVE defect is recordStandalonePayment only: excess = totalAmount - allocatedTotal, unrounded — red-proven 0.010000000000005116 vs 0.01. A roundMoney wrap on the delivery site ships as defence only, not as a fix.
