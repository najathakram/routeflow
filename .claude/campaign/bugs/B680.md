## Reported evidence

duplicate() copies an invoice's line items (verbatim, boxes/pieces/unitsPerBox included per the earlier B644 fix) but never writes a RegulatedSalesLedger SALE row for any regulated-category line on the copy. The original invoice's SALE row exists; the duplicate silently has none, so the regulated ledger under-reports every duplicated regulated sale -- the tax/levy total the ledger is meant to reconcile against master data drifts low by exactly the duplicate's regulated lines.
