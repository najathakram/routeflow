## Reported evidence

Sibling sweep for B246 (grep for useState scanOpen setScanOpen across apps/mobile/app/** and apps/mobile/components/**, per cause-ruling.md section 5): apps/mobile/app/(operator)/purchase-orders/record.tsx declares no scanOpen state and mounts no BarcodeFab or BarcodeScanner of its own - it imports only ProductPickerSheet (line 8, mounted line 207), which owns a scanner behind its own local scanOpen (ProductPickerSheet.tsx line 49). This was the exact pattern cause-ruling.md section 5 predicted (expected: purchase-order receive). To scan a received item the operator must open the product picker first; there is no top-level scan entry on the screen itself, unlike movements.tsx and adjust-picker.tsx which both mount a BarcodeFab directly.
