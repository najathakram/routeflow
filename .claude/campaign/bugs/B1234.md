## Reported evidence

On a PD order a line with deliveredQty 3 of 5 can be cut to 2 or hard-deleted through updateOrderItems (post-delivery edits re-sync in place, never block). The at-door change-request path already refuses the same edit with 409 LINE_ALREADY_DELIVERED. The edit screen leaves the qty input, Not available and Delete item enabled.
