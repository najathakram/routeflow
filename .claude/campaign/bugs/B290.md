## Summary

## What this feature is for

## Root cause

None of the three AP payment writers locks the bill row, so a supplier bill can be paid twice over

Symptom / evidence: Two overlapping payments on a $500 bill create $1,000 of BillPayment rows while totalPaid reads $500 and the bill shows PAID.

Files implicated: apps/api/src/vendor-bills/vendor-bills.service.ts, apps/api/src/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.service.ts.

## User impact

Severity: high. High — a real operational or financial discrepancy that a user or auditor will eventually notice as a books/behavior mismatch.

Symptom: Two overlapping payments on a $500 bill create $1,000 of BillPayment rows while totalPaid reads $500 and the bill shows PAID.

## Fix approach and UX

Suggested test: REG-B<id> every vendor-bill payment writer takes SELECT … FOR UPDATE on the bill and derives totalPaid from the post-insert ledger (filled with this record's own id at filing time).

Coupling constraint: the F35 vendor-bill void/pay/receive guards (A1-conservation#2, A2-state-machines#3, A5-concurrency#3, A5-concurrency#4) land together in one diff — they are three guards on the same VOID/PAID state machine and landing one without the others leaves a bypass through a sibling endpoint.
