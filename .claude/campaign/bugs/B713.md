## Reported evidence

applyScheduledDowngrades (:208-299): prepare() runs outside the transaction, then a bare update applies it. A downgrade cancelled or superseded by a paid upgrade in between still applies at cron time. Cite B412 as the sibling class. Fix: updateMany gated on {planKey, downgradeToPlanKey, downgradeEffectiveAt} matching what prepare() read. Found by MacBook's A5 concurrency verifier. Fast-follow.
