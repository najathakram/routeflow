## Reported evidence

From SA-628o (#2554 5939011834), found in the B965+B967 review (#2988), via Mac Lead. loadOrderPool's legacy itemless term attributes a pre-SA-628 refund's line-unit qty onto invoice lines, so on a box-unaware line it is UNDER-counted in pieces. Under-counting a past credit makes the remaining room look LARGER, which is the unsafe direction (a possible over-credit). It is LOW only because it is confined to legacy (pre-SA-628) itemless refunds on box-unaware lines; raise it if the sizing shows live rows. Fix: convert the legacy term with the same boundary helper (poolPieces / standardReturnPieces), refusing an unknown or multi-factor basis. Money, low.
