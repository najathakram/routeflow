## Reported evidence

When Google Calendar integration is unconfigured or unreachable in prod, getAvailability() returns {days: []} identically to a genuinely fully-booked window. demo-scheduler.tsx renders the same 'No times are available this month... email us' message for both, so visitors and support cannot tell a broken integration from a busy calendar.
