## Fix approach and UX

Fleet lead ruling 2026-09-23 (twice-missed conventions should be mechanical); verified on origin/master 2ed80789: verify-turbo.mjs reads `JEST_MAX_WORKERS = process.env.JEST_MAX_WORKERS || ''` and `VERIFY_CONCURRENCY = process.env.VERIFY_CONCURRENCY || ''` (:72-73); unset means full concurrency. Observed twice tonight on the 16 GB Mac mini (Colima holds 6 GB): a verify without the inline throttle drove kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level to 4 (critical) — R-ui's #1280 run, then Lane B's bare `git push` for #1286 (the pre-push hook calls verify at full concurrency). SPEC (CODE route, not a shell-profile env — editing the mini's profile is persistent machine config needing the owner's OK and is still a per-machine convention): (1) default VERIFY_CONCURRENCY=1 and JEST_MAX_WORKERS=2 when os.totalmem() < 24 GB; (2) explicit env vars still override; (3) print ONE line naming the defaults chosen and why (e.g. 'low-memory host (16 GB < 24 GB): VERIFY_CONCURRENCY=1 JEST_MAX_WORKERS=2'); (4) hosts >= 24 GB (the PC) are byte-identical in behaviour. Pure-logic tests for the threshold decision (extract a pure chooseDefaults(totalmemBytes, env) and pin both sides of 24 GB plus the override). Owner: R-ui (idle on the mini pending #1275/#1280).
