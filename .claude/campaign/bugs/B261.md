## Reported evidence

components/auth/AuthShell.tsx renders the story panel's h2 (line 41) before the card's h1 (line 84) in source/DOM order, on all 15 pages that use AuthShell. A screen-reader user reading top to bottom meets the page's secondary heading before its primary one. This is the visual layout's intended left-to-right story-then-form order (design intent, not an oversight) and the story panel is hidden entirely at 850px and below, so impact is desktop-only and non-blocking.
