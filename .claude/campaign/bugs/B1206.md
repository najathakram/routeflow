## Reported evidence

Reproduced locally at 1440 on master 0dfb5565 (web image): two scans in quick succession (fill+Enter back to back, or the 2nd scan 200 ms after the 1st with a 400 ms lookup) keep only the second line; the first line and its quantity prefix are silently lost (both GET /products calls return 200, console clean). With 100-400 ms lookup latency a fast scanner under-charges the walk-in sale and under-decrements stock. Slow scans (1.5 s apart) and char-by-char wedge typing keep both. Fix direction: queue scans or fetch per scan with its own quantity.
