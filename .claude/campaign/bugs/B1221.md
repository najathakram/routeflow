## Reported evidence

The invoice importer matches customers by businessName without excludeWalkIn, so an invoice row whose customer name matches the walk-in record attaches to the walk-in customer. Mirror the customer-import rule from #3280 (never match the walk-in). Follow-up to #3280, approved by the lead.
