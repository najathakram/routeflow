## Reported evidence

On /company between ~761px and ~950px, the .company-principles grid (label column + .feature-grid.three) overflows its .wrap container — visibly broken layout (large empty gutter, misaligned cards), scrollWidth 862 vs innerWidth 768 at the worst point. Root cause is nested implicit min-width:auto grid blowout at TWO levels: .feature-grid is itself a grid item of .company-principles, and .feature-grid's own article children are grid items of .feature-grid — neither shrinks below its content's min-content by default. A scoped min-width:0 fix at both levels (tested locally) cuts the overflow from 94px to 64px but does not eliminate it; a third, smaller overflow then surfaces in one card's own paragraph text. Underlying architecture issue: .company-principles only collapses to a single column at max-width:760px, while its child .feature-grid already collapses from 3 to 2 columns at max-width:900px — the 761-900px band has both mid-collapse and doubly cramped, which is likely the real fix target (align/raise .company-principles's single-column breakpoint) rather than another min-width patch. Found via a production 390/768/1440 viewport sweep across all 11 marketing routes (2026-09-18); NOT fixed this round — needs its own dedicated investigation, filed with full diagnosis for whoever picks it up.
