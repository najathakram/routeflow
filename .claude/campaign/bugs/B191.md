## Reported evidence

_Imported verbatim from the bug register — this is the ORIGINAL report, not analysis._

**Meant to do.** After the cooldown, presenting the same item again adds a second unit; holding it in frame still adds only once.

**Actually does.** The gate slid its clock on EVERY detection, including rejected per-frame repeats — so holding an item in frame extended suppression indefinitely, and re-presenting at a natural sub-700ms pace never cleared. Operators saw “item added” haptics with quantities that lagged what they scanned.

**The gap.** One timestamp served two jobs: frame-spam suppression and re-present detection. Refreshing it on rejects made the second impossible.

**Evidence.** Evidence · apps/mobile/lib/scan-loop.ts — lastAcceptAt vs lastSeenAt; scan-loop.test.ts pins the 600ms pace as a literal
