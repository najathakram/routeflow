## Reported evidence

From the #3010 Opus review (#2554), via the lead. ContactPerson first and last names are user-typed and reach the writers in customers.service.ts and import.service.ts unguarded, so a formula-led name can reach the verbatim regulated/tobacco filings. Fix: the TEXT_CELL_FORMULA_LEAD guard at those writers, change-only (existing rows untouched), coded 400; the import does a row-reject. API, low.
