## Reported evidence

With freeUnits>0 and freeUnitSize>1 (box-split line stored with a promo snapshot) the floored free-unit telescope makes the curve drop: stored 24.00, 20 pcs, 2 free boxes of 4 - 9 pieces bills 18.00 but 10 pieces bills 12.00. Built through applyBestPromotion -> computeLineSubtotal: 297 of 648 swept lines (upb 2..12, BUY1GET1/2GET1/1GET2/3GET1) have at least one delivered qty that bills less than a smaller one, e.g. upb2 BUY1GET1 8 pcs stored 40: 3 delivered bills 30, 4 bills 20. Partial credits/returns/price-correction targets computed from it are wrong by up to one box price. Found by SA-574 characterization (test tenants only).
