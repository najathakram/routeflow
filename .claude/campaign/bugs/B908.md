## Reported evidence

PO receive and vendor-bill receive/revert/void lock Product rows id-sorted (component C, lower id, then wants recipe R) while recipe and catalog writers hold R and want C (house order FAMILY row, recipe row, then targets): a lock cycle. Postgres aborts one side with 40P01; bill void/revert and PO receive do not retry, so the user gets a 500. Pre-existing, not a #2803 regression; no data corruption. Fix: one house order, either (A) stock paths lock family/recipe rows first or (B) catalog writers take FOR SHARE on rows they only read. Proof: a real-Postgres two-connection harness, no 40P01 in 20 runs. Source: SA-607b (#2554 5918396805); full body in .claude/sa/TICKET-catalog-lock-order.md. Tier 1, concurrency.
