## Root cause

Found by the Opus review of #1287 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); every site verified on origin/master 2ed80789. Money rule: round every monetary write. (1) MEDIUM — advance APPLY: customers.service.ts:1521 `applyAmount = Math.min(Number(ap.balance), invoiceBalance, dto.amount ?? Infinity)` is unrounded; body is `@Body() dto: any` (customers.controller.ts:232, no validation); written as the payment amount (:1528) and the balance decrement (:1537). Advance 20.00, amount 10.005 → payment stores 10.01, balance 9.995 → reads 10.00: 20.01 booked against 20.00 deposited. (2) LOW — recordStandalonePayment writes `amount: alloc.amount` unrounded (invoices.service.ts:5730) and sums raw allocations (:5775); AllocationDto has no maxDecimalPlaces. totalAmount 100.02, allocations 3 × 33.335 → excess 0.02 but payments store 3 × 33.34 = 100.02: 100.04 booked against 100.02. (#1287/B763 rounds only the excess.) (3) LOW — createAdvancePayment writes dto.amount raw (customers.service.ts:~1290), `dto: any` (customers.controller.ts:220): 0.004 passes the <= 0 check and lands as a $0.00 advance row. Fix: roundMoney at each write AND real class-validator DTOs (@IsNumber({ maxDecimalPlaces: 2 }), @IsPositive) for create/apply advance and allocations. Owner: the landing coordinator (owns the customers.service advance area since B310). Money — carve-out parked. Size: invoices.service.ts has 0 slack — site (2) must be net ≤ 0 there.
