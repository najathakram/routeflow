## Reported evidence

LITE tenant navigating to a plan-gated route (e.g. /estimates) briefly sees the real page render (spinner + an inline error) before the lock overlay appears; once settled up to 3 simultaneous PLAN_GATE toasts stack naming different/contradictory tiers for one navigation; a stray toast can also appear on the dashboard with no gated navigation. Root cause: RouteGuard renders LockedPage as a wrapper around the real page (LockedPage gate={...}>{children}</LockedPage>), so the gated page always mounts and fires its data queries even when locked -- LockedPage's ghost/blur treatment only hides it visually. Merged in #777 (master 8f1d7fa1), logged during review as finding 8 (LOW), re-rated HIGH after the post-merge UV Playwright pass confirmed the worse-than-expected behaviour at 1440/768/390.
