## Reported evidence

The helper only string-matches a bare address domain suffix. A recipient such as "Acme Store <acme_store@imported.local>" or one with trailing whitespace passes the guard and reaches a transport. Fix: extract the addr-spec (or trim and parse) before the suffix check, and mirror it in web lib/formatting.ts and mobile lib/internal-email.ts (triple mirror plus the new displayEmail render gate). Follow-up named by the Opus r2 PASS review of PR #2854.
