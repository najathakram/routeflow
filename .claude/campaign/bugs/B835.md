## Root cause

From the #1163 copier-contract work (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23). C17 = invoices.service.ts recomputeOrderFromInvoices; on an invoice line with a productId and no matching order line it CREATES an OrderItem. Per #1163's registry entry (96cbbfd4, copier-registry.ts:214-240) it carries categoryTaxAmount but not categoryTaxType/categoryTaxRate/categoryPriceIncludesTax, so the line holds a tax amount with no snapshot of its basis; a later recompute or filing can't tell which rate produced it. Fix: carry the trio via copyCategoryTaxSnapshot (@routeflow/pricing, as merge-line-collect.ts:126 does) or rederive from the live category by the C6/C7/C8 rule, and list the trio in C17's carried/rederived, with a pin.
