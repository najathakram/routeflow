## Root cause

From the Opus review of #1278 (fleet lead relay 2026-09-23); verified on origin/master 2ed80789: `const deliveries = (stop.orders ?? []).flatMap(o => o.id === orderId && reconciledFirst ? reconciledFirst : (o.lineItems ?? []).map(li => ({ type: 'DELIVERED', quantityDelivered: Number(li.qty ?? 0) })))` (:288-306). Only orders[0] (the screen's orderId) can carry the driver's reconciled short-pick plan; EVERY other order on a multi-order stop is posted as delivered in full with no driver choice, and when orders[0]'s data has not loaded reconciledFirst is null so ITS stored short-pick plan is silently ignored too (delivered in full). Since the payment path bills on deliveredQty, a refused or short second order is billed in full. #1278's step-7 fix adds a refuse-to-close guard for the not-loaded case; the multi-order case needs its own fix (per-order reconcile/choice). Units-gating (medium).
