## Reported evidence

From Mac Lead (lead-approved), a follow-up to B983. B983 guards ProductCategory, TrackedCategory and TrackedSubcategory names at their writers but not the free-text Product.category column, so a formula-led value can still be written by product create, update and import and reach the verbatim regulated/tobacco filings. Fix: assertSafeTextCell on Product.category at the product writers, change-only on update (existing rows untouched), the product import rejects the row, and add a writer-scan GUARDED entry. Lane: U-units, after B983. API, low.
