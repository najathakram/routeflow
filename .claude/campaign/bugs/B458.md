## Reported evidence

The --stamp flag unconditionally runs git rev-parse HEAD and writes it to _meta.json, regardless of what branch or commit HEAD actually is. Run on a feature branch or an abandoned worktree, this stamps mappedSha to a sha that never lands on master -- an 'orphaned mappedSha' that the next session's drift check can't reconcile against real history. Should refuse (or at least warn loudly) when HEAD isn't reachable from origin/master.
